## Where the outcomes branch off

Only endings 1 and 2 finish the main story. Every other outcome is a resting point you can climb back from. What leads to each is in Planning Tasks, task 7; this table adds where on the spine each one can happen.

| # | Outcome | Can happen in | Way back to the main line |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | The lakefront Starflare with Gus | Act 5, opening night | Final |
| 2 | The Starflare without Gus | Act 5, opening night | Final |
| 3 | The Starflare floats | Act 5, if the land falls through | Win the land later and move ashore |
| 4 | The people's Starflare | Act 5, if the land falls through | The regulars pool small shares to reopen on a shoestring, with Gus hosting (no union stake). Players who never get rich settle here; the lakefront still takes your own winnings |
| 5 | Tae's restaurant | Act 4, Tae's offer | Buy the name back from Tae |
| 6 | Gus lets go | Act 4, after helping him quit | Gus comes back for a reopening |
| 7 | The name stays with Starflare Field | Act 4, Field timer ends first | Win the name back later by leverage |
| 8 | Mob takeover | Act 4, Wren's deal | Expose Wren later to take the name (leads to 2) |
| 9 | Burned down | Act 3 or 4, fighting dirty with high heat | Carry on as normal; stay clear of Revaris for a while before backers trust you |
| 10 | The sellout | Act 4 (Wren) or act 5 (Tae) | Leverage can still take the name (2), or a long climb on generous and reliable (1) |

The ways back were agreed with Mike on 2026-10-21. Relighting the rocket always means a brand-new sign for the new Starflare, so the old sign's fate (stadium gate, fire, or Tae's restaurant in outcome 5) is flavour only.
