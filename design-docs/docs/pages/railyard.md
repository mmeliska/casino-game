## New venue: the RailYard

The RailYard fills the gap between the Flare ($5 to $100) and the Harborlight ($25 to $1,000). Mike named it on 2026-10-19; the details below are proposals.

| District | Games | Bets | Twist | Getting in |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| The Levee, the old rail depot at its edge | The core five, video poker, keno; rail-themed slots | $10–$500 | Players' card with points and small comps; weeknight slot tournaments | Reach $1,500 once |

It's clean, busy and a little plain, with a buffet, and the mill pensioners love it. It shows what the Flare could be if Gus ran it well. Most regulars land here after the fall, and the rival shows off here early. No casino or well-known game uses the name; Santa Fe and Texarkana have "Railyard" districts, but it's a common word.
