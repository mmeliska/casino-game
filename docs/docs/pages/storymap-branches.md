## Where the outcomes branch off

**Clean or dirty (Mike, 2026-10-26).** Act 4 has no cash route; leverage wins the name back. White-hat leverage (proof of real wrongdoing that never hurts an innocent person) is the clean road. Every act 4 route also has a black-hat version that works faster or cheaper, because the game always lets you take advantage of a situation for selfish reasons: trade your silence to Wren for the name ({A4.3}), lean on the payers named in Raffy's drive (with Ray, or on your own once you've rebuilt the key), have the trust-account records lifted instead of subpoenaed, or lean on Richard instead of earning his trust (2026-10-28, 2026-10-30). It wins the name, and the game remembers: heat goes up, your crooked or ruthless lean grows, Gus, Dot, Rosa, Ed and Richard cool on you, Wren takes notice, the license hearing ({A5.7}) weighs it and sets stricter conditions, and it leans toward the Starflare without Gus ({O2}) unless you repair things. Taking Wren's early offer on the Ed route is clean by this rule (no blackmail); its costs are Ray and Wren's freedom, shown in the epilogue (Mike, 2026-10-30).

Only endings 1 and 2 finish the main story. Every other outcome is a resting point you can climb back from, except 9, a setback you play through (Mike, 2026-10-29). There is no leaving ending: the drive-away was cut (Mike, 2026-10-27). What leads to each is in Planning Tasks, task 7; this table adds where on the spine each one can happen.
