## Where the outcomes branch off

**Clean or dirty (Mike, 2026-10-26).** Act 4 has no cash route; leverage wins the name back. White-hat leverage (proof of real wrongdoing that never hurts an innocent person) is the clean road. Every act 4 route also has a black-hat version that works faster or cheaper, because the game always lets you take advantage of a situation for selfish reasons: blackmail with Wren's payers list, hand Ed the whole drive with the names, have the trust-account records lifted instead of subpoenaed, or lean on Richard instead of earning his trust. It wins the name, and the game remembers: heat goes up, your crooked or ruthless lean grows, Gus, Dot, Rosa, Ed and Richard cool on you, Wren takes notice, the license hearing ({A5.7}) weighs it and sets stricter conditions, and it leans toward the Starflare without Gus ({O2}) unless you repair things.

Only endings 1 and 2 finish the main story. Every other outcome is a resting point you can climb back from. What leads to each is in Planning Tasks, task 7; this table adds where on the spine each one can happen.
