## The districts

Northlight has five districts, poorest to richest, and the climb from one to the next is a climb from sodium streetlight to chandelier. A player should know where they are from one glance at a background.

| District | What it is | Key light | Palette | Recurring image | Weather layer |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Backwater | The dry district across the river; voted no on gaming, so every game here is illegal. Mill families, back rooms, street dice | Sodium streetlamp, bar neon, bowling-alley sign | Amber, brick red, tired green | The rocket sign across the river from the motel lot | Rain on asphalt, steam from mill grates |
| The Bridges | The main crossing; gas-station slots | Gas-station canopy fluorescents | Flat white, oil-slick purple | The Long Bridge's lights over black water | Fog before dawn |
| The Levee | Old downtown on the river; the Starflare, the riverboats, the Stacks | The rocket sign, flickering | Teal, coral, chrome, rust | The Stacks against the sky; the bucket under the Flare's roof leak | River mist, the Prairie Queen's paddle lights |
| The Marquee Mile | Neon lakefront strip; hotel casinos and new money | Hotel marquees, LED walls | Magenta, gold, lake blue | The North Light sweeping the strip | Wind off the lake, winter snow |
| Silvershore | Old money; private clubs, resorts, yachts | Warm lamps, candlelight | Ivory, deep green, brass | Yachts on a dark lake, the city glowing far off | Clear and still; no neon is the luxury |

- **Noir by night, warm by day.** Northlight is a city people like. Daylight is warm and busy (pierogi at Kasia's, league night at Millstone Lanes, the lakefront in summer); the grit belongs to the hours after the tables close.
- **Weather and seasons are free mood layers.** A static rain, fog or snow overlay changes a reused background more than a new pose would.
- **Props carry history.** The city's past is never a paragraph: it's the old meeting sign on Kasia's back-room door, Pete's 25-year rocket pin, the Stacks outside every Levee window.
- **Match the big compositions.** The rocket sign seen from Dot's motel lot on night one, and the city seen from Vivian's yacht later with the rocket a tiny coral dot across the water, show how far the player has climbed.

## The cameras

Revaris cameras cover the city (Mike, 2026-10-26): casinos, bars, shops, motels and parking lots take them below worth, and the city's "Safe Streets" contract puts Revaris plate readers on streets and bridges (Bennett championed it; Ed's squad uses it). Every venue keeps its own copies; the city readers feed Revaris directly. Backwater voted only on casinos, not cameras: its streets have the readers and most of its shops took Revaris cameras. Three places refuse them, each for its own reasons and with no tie between them: Kasia's Tap (her back room), Millstone Lanes (Juan's dice and his regulars) and the Slack Water Motel (Dot's guests' privacy). The Long Bridge stays dark, because the hoodlums keep smashing its readers (Mike, 2026-10-30). What happens inside the three places reaches Wren only through her people. See [wren].
