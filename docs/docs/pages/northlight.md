## The districts

Northlight has five districts, poorest to richest, and the climb from one to the next is a climb from sodium streetlight to chandelier. A player should know where they are from one glance at a background.

| District | What it is | Key light | Palette | Recurring image | Weather layer |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Backwater | The dry district across the river; voted no on gaming, so every game here is illegal. Mill families, back rooms, street dice | Sodium streetlamp, bar neon, bowling-alley sign | Amber, brick red, tired green | The rocket sign across the river from the motel lot | Rain on asphalt, steam from mill grates |
| The Bridges | The main crossing; gas-station slots | Gas-station canopy fluorescents | Flat white, oil-slick purple | The Long Bridge's lights over black water | Fog before dawn |
| The Levee | Old downtown on the river; the Starflare, the riverboats, the Stacks | The rocket sign, flickering | Teal, coral, chrome, rust | The Stacks against the sky; the bucket under the Flare's roof leak | River mist, the Prairie Queen's paddle lights |
| The Marquee Mile | Neon lakefront strip; hotel casinos and new money | Hotel marquees, LED walls | Magenta, gold, lake blue | The North Light sweeping the strip | Wind off the lake, winter snow |
| Silvershore | Old money; private clubs, resorts, yachts | Warm lamps, candlelight | Ivory, deep green, brass | Yachts on a dark lake, the city glowing far off | Clear and still; no neon is the luxury |

- **Noir by night, warm by day.** Northlight is a city people like. Daylight is warm and busy (pierogi at Kasia's, league night at Millstone Lanes, the lakefront in summer); the grit belongs to the hours after the tables close.
- **Weather and seasons are free mood layers.** A static rain, fog or snow overlay changes a reused background more than a new pose would.
- **Props carry history.** The city's past is never a paragraph: it's the old meeting sign on Kasia's back-room door, Pete's 25-year rocket pin, the Stacks outside every Levee window.
- **Match the big compositions.** The rocket sign seen from Dot's motel lot on night one, and the lakefront towers seen from Vivian's yacht later, framed the same way, show how far the player has climbed. The Flare is inland on the river, so the yacht can't see the rocket sign (Mike, 2026-10-31).

## The city map

Northlight has one illustrated map. You look at it and tap a place to travel there.

**Settled (Mike, 2026-11-02):** the map is on your phone. Every screen outside the games has a map button that opens it, and it shows only the places open to you. Until the icons exist, each place is a circle with its name beside it.

**Settled (Mike, 2026-11-02):** the lake is a Great Lake and the map shows it like an ocean shore, with no far shore. It lies along the city's north and east sides. The river runs down the middle of the city from the lake in the north. The map is an image the player views and travels from. A canal runs from the river to the lake and is the Marquee Mile's southern border, so the Mile is bounded by water on every side: the river on the west, the lake on the north and east, and the canal on the south. The only way onto the Mile is a bridge. Backwater has a clear western edge with open country beyond it, and the river sits west of centre, so the Levee and the Mile are the biggest districts. Where the canal leaves the river, the river bends: it turns west for a short stretch in line with the canal, then south again, so from the motel the water seems to run straight on east into the canal. The Long Bridge spans that westward stretch, from the motel's bank to the Levee. The Flare stands on the Levee's waterfront where the river meets the canal, across the water from the motel. The Slack Water Motel sits on the north bank of that stretch, and the First Levee Bank faces it from the south bank.

**The layout (proposal).** Backwater is on the west bank, between the river and a highway lined with trees that marks the city's western edge. Beyond the highway is open country: farms, fields and pines, where the roads out to the excursions run. The Levee faces it on the east bank, inland on the river. The Bridges are the crossings between them. The Long Bridge, the main one, spans the bend by the motel, with Bridgehead, the pawn shop and the underpass where you sleep in your car at its Backwater foot. The Marquee Mile runs along the north lakefront from the harbour mouth, where the North Light stands, to the corner where the shore turns south and down to the canal. Silvershore runs down the east shore south of the canal. Vivian's land is the Mile's last lot, on the shore at the canal's mouth, with Silvershore across the water. The canal has a lock partway along it, with Lock Street Station beside it on the Levee side. Three bridges lead onto the Mile: the top river bridge from Backwater, and two canal bridges, one from the Levee and one from Silvershore. The order runs west to east, poorest to richest. Three settled facts drive it: Backwater is across the river from the Levee, the Flare is inland where the yacht can't see it, and Vivian's land sits at the Silvershore line. The final base map is `Images/Map/city_map.png` (4096 px). `city_map_layout.png` beside it is the layout sketch it was made from, and `city_map_pins.png` draws the numbered pins below over the final map so they can be checked. `tools/map_layout.py` redraws both.

**How it's built (Mike, 2026-11-02).** The map is two layers:

- **A flat base map.** Plain flat colour with no buildings: the lake, the river and canal, the bridges, the districts, a few main streets.
- **An illustrated icon for each place.** A small building in the game's inked style, laid over the base at the pin positions below. The icon is the button you tap. The game adds the place's name when you select it.

Places can open, change or close without a new map. A place that changes swaps its icon:

- The Flare's site goes from the Flare to the burned shell (O9), then the construction site from the ground-breaking (A3.5), then Starflare Field.
- Vivian's land becomes the lakefront Starflare.
- The south pier is the Starflare afloat (O3).
- The Aurelian's icon also stands for Tae's restaurant (O5).

The map is square, so it pans and pinch-zooms the same way in portrait and landscape.
