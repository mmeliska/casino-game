The room 9 mystery moves into Gus's time: Gus's Umbra camera deal, Bennett's leverage over it, and the box Gus gave Richard. Approved by Mike and applied on 2026-10-22 to Planning Tasks, the Story Map, Northlight Locations and the Character Bible.

## The new truth

In story order, all a few years before the game starts:

1. **The camera deal (yours).** The gaming commission requires a modern camera system, and the Flare can't afford one. Wren's security company offers cheap AI cameras plus cash up front. Wren invented the cameras, and she and her grandfather, still Umbra's boss then, land the deal together; it's what makes him hand her the reins (Mike, 2026-10-25). Gus signs knowing the company is Umbra's front, because it keeps the doors open. It's his second secret, next to his gambling.
2. **Richard's markers.** Richard ran up markers at the Flare's tables. The house he'd lived in for decades went to cover them, and he still owed more.
3. **Bennett finds out (yours).** As the alderman who holds the Flare's license, Bennett traces the camera company through business and license records, then links it to Umbra through his contacts at the police department.
4. **The squeeze.** Bennett forces Gus to wipe out what Richard still owes, in return for keeping quiet about the cameras. His dislike of the Flare is now personal: it took his father's house.
5. **The proof.** Gus, ashamed, gives Richard the key to a safety deposit box at an old Levee bank. Gus rented the box in Richard's name, so it opens only with the key and Richard's own signature at the bank. Inside are the original signed camera contract (Wren's own copy went in a shredder once Gus signed) and Gus's own recording of Bennett making the demand in Gus's office. Bennett picked the office because it is off the floor cameras, so Wren's system never saw it; Gus ran a recorder in his desk. It is the only copy. Gus tells him: "If anyone ever comes after you over the debt, open it." Richard wears the key on a lanyard around his neck and never takes it off. He has never opened the box: nobody has come after him, he keeps his word, and he's too proud to take help from the man whose casino took his house. The key is a reminder, not a tool.
6. **Room 9.** Richard ends up at the Slack Water. Bennett pays the room every week by envelope and never visits. Richard thinks Gus pays it out of guilt.
7. **Why Umbra watches room 9.** The box proves that Wren's camera company, Revaris Technologies, is Umbra's front. Wren lacks both things in it: the original signed contract and the only recording. Taking the key off an old man gets her nothing, because the box is in his name and needs his signature, so her people watch room 9 to see if he ever goes to the bank, and who he talks to. (Audit 3.2.)

**Proposal, Ed's part:** Ed is the police contact, and he gave the Umbra link to Bennett in good faith, thinking the alderman would help build a case against Wren. Bennett used it for himself instead. That keeps Ed honest and gives him a reason to distrust Bennett. Ed never learns what Bennett did with it unless you tell him.

## How the mystery plays

**Clues (any order):**

- Dot sees a driver drop an envelope at her desk every week (unchanged).
- A photo of Richard sits on Bennett's office desk (unchanged).
- Cards with Richard: he talks about the house he lost at the Flare and says Gus "takes care of" his room. That points you at Gus first, a small false lead. The game is War, one pass (Mike, 2026-10-22): 10 cards each, every card flipped once, won cards go to a score pile instead of back into play, and the most cards wins, so it never runs past 10 cards. Ties use standard War rules (Mike, 2026-10-22): each puts three cards face down and one face up, and the higher face-up card takes them all. If either player has fewer than four cards left, they flip their last card face up instead. Each visit is one game, a few lines of his story and a nudge to his trust. Proposal: it's the game he taught Bennett as a boy. It happens at a picnic table in the Slack Water lot (Mike, 2026-10-22). War replaces gin rummy everywhere (Story Map act 3 "Clues come together", Planning Tasks task 8, Northlight Locations).

**Solved:** once Richard trusts you, you're the one who notices Umbra watching his door and tells him. Someone is coming after him, so Gus's condition is met, and he opens the box with you. He learns two things at once: his son has paid his room all along, and his son used Gus's secret to clear his debt.

**Richard's choice (he has the final say):**

| Choice | What happens |
| --- | --- |
| Shame Bennett | Richard threatens to release the footage unless Bennett amends his stadium ordinance to return the Starflare name to Gus. It works, but Bennett's grudge hardens. |
| Reconcile | Richard gives Bennett the box and they talk for the first time in years. Out of gratitude, Bennett amends his ordinance and the name goes back to Gus. |
| Burn it | The leverage road closes; you win the name back another way. |
| **Hand it to Ed (Mike: in)** | Ed gets his case against Wren's cameras (the informant path), but Gus's Umbra deal comes out too, which hurts Gus's trust and the Flare's name. |

**The new catch:** any use of the box in public exposes Gus as well as Bennett, so the leverage route to winning back the name costs Gus something. The private choices (shame, reconcile) avoid that.
