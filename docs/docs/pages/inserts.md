## How this page works

The prompts below are read from the pages they belong to when this page opens: a character's props from their page under **Insert prompts**, a place's props from its page under **Plate prompts**, and the walk-ons' props from [the cast page's walk-ons](#cast). To change a prompt, edit it on its own page; this page follows.

Every insert is a square 1:1 close-up. The how-to, the settings and the three negative prompts (inserts, inserts with a hand in frame, and photographs with people in them) are on [Plate prompts](#plates). Each note says when an insert needs the hand or photo one. Each tag is also the image's file name under `Assets/Resources/Inserts` and the scene's `# insert:` tag.
