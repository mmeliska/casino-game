The player is a blank slate. They arrive by car at night with about $200, set their gender on Dot's register card at check-in (the rival Chase or Brooke matches it, and it sets which wedding dance is offered first), and are defined only by four hidden scales: generous/tight, reliable/flaky, ruthless/soft, straight/crooked. They enter their name when they sign Dot's register at check-in (A1.1), and characters use it from then on. Their romance preference is set by what they do at the wedding at Kasia's Tap (A1.4).
