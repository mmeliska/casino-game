*Core · new money · the Aurelian, Gilded Pine*

## Look

A Korean-American man in his early 40s; a sharp charcoal suit, no tie, sleeves never wrinkled, North Light lighthouse cufflinks.

## Backstory

His parents came to the Levee when he was small, a drummer and a seamstress, and the Flare's house band was the only steady gig in a city that was running out of them. He grew up in a second-floor flat on the Flare's block, doing his homework to the band through the wall while his father drummed in it, and learned two things early: the Flare was the warmest room in the Levee, and it paid his father late. His father played there eleven years. Tae hung around the floor as a kid because Gus let him, and later he was the big brother little Stella followed around the lounge. He's an old family friend of Gus's family; no romance (Mike, 2026-10-28).

When the band folded, his father's drum kit stayed behind in the Flare's basement for the next band. There never was one. The kit is still there, under a tarp, behind the one-armed bandits the Cinder Room didn't take. His father died with a Flare paycheck stub in his wallet from the band's last week. Tae knows the kit is there and has never been down to the basement. Years ago he asked Gus once if it was still there, and Gus said "Anyway. Coffee?" (Mike, 2026-10-28)

He left for college on a scholarship and came back for his father's funeral with the out-of-town chain's name on his business card and a plan for his city he'd been drawing since the band folded. The chain sent him home because he asked, and because the Aurelian's numbers in a Great Lakes casino city were better than anyone expected, which was his pitch. He has a soft spot for the Flare he'd never admit (Mike, 2026-10-28).

## Role

The Aurelian's general manager and the Flare's rival in vision, not business (the Flare can't compete with the Aurelian). The Aurelian, lobby included, opens to the player at $10,000 in act 2 (Mike, 2026-10-30). He wants to buy Gus out fairly so Gus can rest, and to own the Starflare name for a restaurant inside the Aurelian (O5). Only in that outcome does he also get the old rocket sign, from the District's storage or the Starflare Field gate; if the Flare burned (O9), he hangs only the band photo (Mike, 2026-10-29). Before the bailout, with Stella's numbers, he offers to buy the Flare and run it as an Aurelian satellite with Gus kept on as host, so Gus can step back for his health. The offer is sincere. Gus refuses to sell his dad's place to a chain and sells to "a friend of Ray's" (really Umbra) instead. He opposes bulldozing the Flare for the stadium. He believes the city deserves a world-class casino, that Gus is killing himself for a building, and that the building is the best thing the Levee ever made, which is why he wants the name and not the lot. He has never reconciled the two and doesn't try; he builds. Gus and Tae both love the city but want different futures for it: a rivalry of visions, not a villain. A false lead in M2 (he says he would have bought the Flare openly). Hires Pete away at the fall and bids against you for Vivian's land, because the Aurelian needs a tower and because, if the Starflare is going to rise on the lake, part of him wants it to be his. Not a backer.

**Contradiction:** a man who never lies has one subject he never raises. His rivalry is a bid to build the kind of house his father could have been proud to play in. He can't say that, so he says "Northlight deserves world-class," and means his father did (Mike, 2026-10-28).

**The kit** (Mike, 2026-10-28):
- **S4, sharpened.** When you say the drummer's name at the Aurelian, his guard drops as settled, and he asks one question: "Is the kit still there?" If you go and look (the Flare's basement, one plate), it is.
- **The last night (A3.3).** The kit is one of the things that has to leave the building. You can get it to Tae (a job: a flatbed, the trucker if S3 fired) or let the District's crew haul it with the rest. If Tae gets it, it sits in a tarp in the Aurelian's loading dock for a season, because he doesn't know what to do with it either. It's a scene, not an inventory item.
- **Payoff.** In O5 the kit is on a riser under the band photo. In O1, if the house band reunion fires, his father's kit is on the showroom stage, and Tae is in the room and says nothing, which from Tae is a speech.

The Aurelian is a friendly rival, never an ally (Mike, 2026-10-26): in act 2 a Revaris rep pitches Tae a state-of-the-art AI camera system and corporate pushes him to take it, so he does (S13). It is the one decision he makes because corporate told him to, and the one he'll regret (Mike, 2026-10-28). Warn him and he remembers you were right, which softens him in act 5. If Revaris's link to Umbra goes public in act 4, the Aurelian has to rip the cameras out at the start of act 5 and takes a reputation hit, which tips Vivian toward you for the land; the new Starflare opens with its own cameras, never Revaris's (Mike, 2026-10-26).

## Voice

Confident, sincere, salesman's warmth; the city's future; never insults the Flare. Doesn't lie at all, which is what makes him dangerous; he's never needed to, because the city he wants is better than the one it has. Never brings up his father's kit unless you say the drummer's name first (S4). *"Northlight deserves a world-class house. Gus deserves to rest."*
