## Backstory

A tech genius and the old Black Hand boss's granddaughter, whom nobody took seriously; the family expected a grandson or one of the uncles to inherit. Once in charge, she renamed the outfit from the Black Hand to Revaris, after her company (Mike, 2026-10-23). The old guard resent her for it. She keeps the Cinder Room open out of sentiment or for laundering. Revaris Technologies buys the Flare from Gus and puts its land into the Levee Stadium District, making it the District's biggest investor: a clean asset for her, and her cameras in the stadium (2026-10-23).

## The business

High-end AI face-matching cameras installed in the Flare, the RailYard and the Prairie Queen (the Aurelian runs its own) at a price well below their worth, because the real payment is the data they record. She has dirt on Bennett taking casino money, and her camera in Gus's office caught him squeezing Gus, which is why she let Ray's name front his purchase of the Flare. Her cameras caught Bennett taking his hidden cut of the stadium deal, so she owns the man running for mayor.
