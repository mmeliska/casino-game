## Backstory

A tech genius and the old Umbra boss's granddaughter, whom nobody took seriously; the family expected a grandson or one of the uncles to inherit. She left, made her first money by exploiting people through technology (crypto scams in her early 20s), then invented her own AI face-matching cameras and built a legitimate-looking security company around them, Revaris Technologies. About four years before the game she came back and worked with her grandfather to land the Flare's camera deal: he brought the relationships, she brought the technology. The data it brought in, from the Flare and then the RailYard and the Prairie Queen, was worth more than anything the uncles ran, so about two years before the game her grandfather handed her the reins over their heads. She kept what her cameras had caught on the old bosses and half the Machine, which is why nobody pushes back (Mike, 2026-10-25). The outfit is still called Umbra; Revaris Technologies keeps its own name and looks like a respectable tech firm, and nobody outside knows it's Umbra's front (Mike, 2026-10-25). The old guard resent her. She keeps the Cinder Room open out of sentiment or for laundering. Revaris Technologies buys the Flare from Gus and puts its land into the Levee Stadium District, making it the District's biggest investor: a clean asset for her, and her cameras in the stadium (2026-10-23).
