*Supporting · Backwater · the Slack Water Motel desk*

## Look

Grey hair, a headband, an open Hawaiian-style shirt over a white T-shirt, jeans rolled at the ankles, white socks and gym shoes.

## Backstory

Owns the Slack Water Motel and works the desk herself. Slightly older, sympathetic and in the know. Her late sister was Gus's wife, so she is Gus's sister-in-law and Stella's aunt. She and Gus haven't really spoken since the funeral. That is a reveal (a framed photo of the two sisters in Gus's office), never part of her intro.

## Her stories

The flood (she let everyone sleep upstairs free and the dockworkers brought food, hence her soft spot for union people like Rosa); the man in room 9 (a quiet guest who has lived there the last couple of years; his bill comes in an envelope every week, she doesn't know his story, and he never takes off a key on a lanyard around his neck; he is Richard, and Umbra watches his door); and, once she trusts you, the shirt (a souvenir of the one trip she took after her own big win).

## Her warnings

Ray's loans, the dice behind Millstone Lanes when the vice squad is sweeping, the Cinder Room, and carrying winnings over the Long Bridge before dawn.

## Voice

Chatty, motherly, a story for everything. Gossips about everyone, never talks about Gus. She doesn't lie; she warns sideways. *"I'd stay on this side of the river tonight, hon. No reason."*
