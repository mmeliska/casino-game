## Look

Stocky, a thin, neatly trimmed beard (not a mustache), short sandy-grey hair combed neat, a dark three-piece suit with the vest always buttoned, a gold 25-year rocket pin from Gus's father, reading glasses on his head, a rating card in hand.

## Backstory

Lithuanian-American, about 60. Grew up in the Levee alongside Gus; his grandparents and dad worked the steel mills, but the founder gave him his first job dealing at 19 and he has never worked anywhere else. Best man at Gus's wedding, kept the Flare running after the funeral, and the only one who tells Gus hard truths.

## Role

The Flare's face for comps, credit and higher limits; watches your hot streaks. Tae keeps trying to hire him away. Caught between Gus and Stella, a quiet go-between for Dot and Gus, and your way in to Gus.

## Leaving at the fall

Tell Gus yourself, push Pete to do it, or say nothing. He hands you his gold rocket pin; when you open a floor, giving it back hires him home, unless you sold out Gus.
