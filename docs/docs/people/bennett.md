When he traced Gus's camera deal to Umbra (through records, with the Umbra link from Ed), he used it to force Gus to wipe out what Richard still owed. He gave Mitch the bad tip.

He doesn't buy the Flare himself (changed 2026-10-23). He wants a domed baseball stadium, Starflare Field, to revive the Levee, and runs for mayor on "clean up Northlight" against Mayor Dabareski while taking the same money. He keeps the old rocket sign over the Field gate. When the Flare is cleared he finds Gus's surveillance tape of Rosa's table and keeps it secret. An obstacle you negotiate with, never defeat.
