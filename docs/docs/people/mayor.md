## Look

A woman in her late 60s with a big laugh, a red blazer and a photographer always nearby.

## Backstory

The casino mayor, in office forever. She brought in the riverboats and land casinos after the mills closed and is openly friendly with the casino business and the Aurelian. Seen rarely. The name is a wink at "Da Bears" and the one exception to first names only.

## Role

Bennett's boss in the Machine and his quiet target; their mayor's race drifts in the background and lands on the epilogue's newspaper front page.

## Voice

Big, booming and folksy; always on, always shaking hands, laughs before you finish the joke. Slightly arrogant: she thinks the world revolves around her and turns every topic back to herself. Talks about the mills, "jobs", "this great city" and the casinos that saved it; calls everyone "friend". Never talks about the casino money and never names Bennett in public. Lies with a story about the old days. "Clean up Northlight? Honey, I built Northlight." (Settled 2026-10-23.)
