*Core · Backwater · Kasia's Tap*

## Look

Dark hair in a braid, a bar towel over her shoulder, a flannel shirt with the sleeves rolled up, her dad's old mill union pin on the collar.

## Backstory

Mid-40s. Born in Backwater to a mill family, a Polish-American dad and a Mexican-American mom. Took over her dad's tavern, where the mill shifts drank before the mills closed. Knows everyone's family and remembers the town before the casinos.

## Personality

Warm but tough. Runs a tight bar, gives credit but keeps a ledger and remembers every cent, quick to defend her regulars, slow to forgive a skipped debt. Her credit is how the player feels the reliable/flaky scale.

## Her bar

"for amusement only" machines she quietly pays out from the till, pull tabs under the counter, and a back-room card game she invites you into once you've done a couple of jobs for her (A1.3).

## Voice

Brisk, warm, bar talk; weather, the mill and the regulars; never discusses the back room in front of strangers. Lies with a joke. *"Your tab's in the book. The book never forgets. Neither do I."*
