*Romance · the Levee · the Flare's half-empty lounge*

## Look

- **Miles:** Black, mid-30s, close-cropped hair and a trim beard, a vintage tux jacket with a loosened bow tie, his grandfather's old wristwatch, an easy smile; hums while he plays.
- **Audrey (Mike):** Early 30s, purple hair in pinned-up curls, round glasses, an emerald vintage-style dress, dry wit.

## Plot role

Plays the lounge nightly, knows every regular's song and overhears everything. Loves the Flare; the piano gets covered as it declines. Can play the new Starflare's showroom once you own a floor. Table event (proposal): the song request.

## Romance arc

**In short:** Values loyalty. Story role: sees Bennett's people take the tape at the fall (M1) and, if together, helps you get it back. Exclusive route: the house band reunion, after which Tae sells the Starflare name at a fair price. Deal-breaker: selling Gus out.

*The rest of this section is from Romance Arcs: a proposal unless marked settled.*

**First met:** the Flare's half-empty lounge, act 1. **Value: loyalty.** They love the Flare and its people, and they overhear everything, so they notice who stands by whom.

**Story role: the missing tape (M1, the torn patch).** The night the Flare is cleared at the fall, they're packing up the piano and see Bennett's people (city inspectors on the condemnation walk-through) carry the surveillance tape out of Gus's office. **Together:** they tell you, and on a later date they help you get it back (they still play private events at Bennett's fundraisers, so they can get you into the room where it's kept). That answers how you recover the tape, which was still open. **Friend:** they tell you someone took a tape box from the office, but not who. You have to work out it was Bennett. **Ended:** they say nothing, and the tape takes much longer to trace.

**Exclusive route: the house band reunion (together only).** Tae's dad played drums in the Flare's rocket-age house band. Only the piano player can track down the old members and get them on one stage at the new Starflare. Tae comes, hears his dad's songs, and lets the Starflare name go for a fair price instead of a premium: a gentler version of the "buy the name back from Tae" way back (outcome 5).
