*Minor · the Bridges · Bridgehead Loan & Pawn, next to Bridgehead Fuel & Food*

## Look

Male, white, stocky but strong, about 5'9", tattoos on his arms (Mike, 2026-10-23).

## Role

New character (Mike, 2026-10-23) for the inventory system (task 13). He runs Bridgehead Loan & Pawn in the Bridges, next to Bridgehead Fuel & Food, between where you sleep and where you gamble (Mike, 2026-10-24). Buzzer door, cage counter, open until 3am. He buys your items for a fraction and holds each one a few days so you can buy it back. Some items are worth far more to one person.

He is a functional NPC with a portrait and no story, ties or codes, on purpose (Mike, 2026-10-30). In the cast count he's minor, with portrait, alongside Richard and the three Flare regulars. Two things pass over his counter that matter to other people:

- **Gus's lucky chip.** Gus presses one of the Flare's own chips into your hand on its last night (A3.3). After the closing it can't be cashed anywhere, so it's worth nothing except to him, and Troy buys it for almost nothing (Mike, 2026-10-30).
- **What the rival pawns.** If the rival steals from you (A4.8), you find the item behind his glass with a buy-back date, the rival's watch on the shelf beside it. He doesn't say who sold it (`suggestions/S-031`, Mike, 2026-10-28; see `people/chase.md`).

## Voice

Short and flat; names a number, then waits, and never haggles first. Says "It's worth what someone pays" and never asks why you're selling. Lies by pricing low and blaming the market, but is honest about the buy-back date. *"Two hundred. You got till Thursday to want it back."*
