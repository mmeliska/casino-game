## Look

A typical Chicago-style cop: white, late 40s, slightly overweight, a big bushy horseshoe mustache, a rumpled sport coat, a loosened tie, a badge clipped to his belt, always a coffee or a Stacker sandwich in hand.

## Backstory

Honest to the bone, a bit gruff, tired of watching the big fish get away. Works out of the Northlight vice squad at Lock Street Station (Mike, 2026-10-23), next to the old canal lock; "Lock Street" doubles as slang for the lockup.

## Role

The face of the heat system. Illegal games raise heat: low means they're around and know you, medium means warnings and sweeps that shut a game for the night, high means a raid (lose the table money, pay a fine or spend a night in lockup, and the venue goes quiet). He doesn't care about your dice game; he wants Wren for illegal surveillance and blackmail and leans on you to become an informant. He once gave Bennett the Revaris link behind the Flare's cameras in good faith, and Bennett used it for himself.

## Lowering heat

It cools one step for each week you play only licensed venues; Vivian's lawyer clears a raid for a fee; or a tip to Ed drops it at once but starts the informant path, and Revaris may notice.

## Voice

Gruff, tired, cop shorthand; names and favours; never threatens directly. Lies by playing dumber than he is. *"I don't care about your dice. I care who you owe."*
