## Backstory

Black old money, three generations old. Her grandfather founded the family's insurance company: as a young man he wrote policies for the furnace men and dock crews the lakefront firms wouldn't touch, collected door to door in Backwater and the Levee, and paid out when the others found reasons not to. By the time Vivian was a girl the company owned half of Silvershore and the parcel at the line. He took his granddaughter, not his sons, to the card games at his club, because she could sit still and count (Mike, 2026-10-28).

Her father and uncles sold the company to a national firm, kept the land, and moved the family's name onto a foundation and off the policies. Vivian married a man from the Mile who liked that she was old money and left when he found out what it cost to keep. No children; her heir is a nephew in another state who sends a card, and his lawyers. She has run the family's affairs for thirty years and watched it become smaller and more respectable every one of them (Mike, 2026-10-28).

She isn't on a losing streak. She is the last of the family, and she is spending its money down on purpose, with grace, at the tables, rather than leave it to the nephew's lawyers. The tables are the last place in Northlight where her family's money does what it was for: it goes to the house, the house pays the dealers, and the dealers are the grandchildren of the men on the policies. She is very good at losing because she has made it a discipline. The lakefront land is the one thing she won't let the lawyers have, and she is holding it for whoever asks what it should become (Mike, 2026-10-28). She has no tie to the Flare's founder (Mike, 2026-10-28). She also fills the Silvershore family head role.

## Role

She looks like a high roller on a losing streak, and she is your door into Silvershore. You meet her at the Gilded Pine, which is open in every season, any time your best bankroll reaches $25,000 (Mike, 2026-10-30). She sponsors you into the Obsidian (at $50,000, with a suit) and, after A3.6, invites you to the yacht games, which also need about $100,000 (Mike, 2026-10-30).

Her family owns the lakefront land the new Starflare needs, and how you treat her decides whether you or Tae gets it. Tae has asked for it with a cheque; nobody has asked her what it should become. At A5.1 the land keeps its standing-based pricing, with one hidden option: before any price is named, the player can ask her what she'd want the land to be. If they do, she tells them and lowers the price one step. Nothing in the UI flags it; a player who leads with a number gets the number (Mike, 2026-10-28). If Umbra was exposed in act 4, the Aurelian rips out its Revaris cameras and the reputation hit tips her your way.

One of two backers, with Jalen. She is a silent minority partner in the new Starflare, with a share equal to what she paid in; the player owns it and holds the license (Mike, 2026-10-30).
