## Look

Early 60s, a sleek silver bob, pearls, a camel coat, dark sunglasses indoors.

## Backstory

Black old money. Her great-grandfather founded an insurance company that covered the mill and dock workers nobody else would insure, and the family fortune grew from it. She also fills the Silvershore family head role.

## Role

A high roller on a losing streak and your door into Silvershore: she sponsors you into the Obsidian and invites you to the Gilded Pine and the yachts. Her family owns the lakefront land the new Starflare needs, and her losses push her to sell, so how you treat her decides whether you or Tae gets it. One of only two backers (a silent partner). Her lawyer can clear a raid off your record for a fee and can pull Rosa's commission file.
