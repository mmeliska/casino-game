**Already decided:** short, milestone-unlocked trips, each a setting for a story arc or special game. Ideas: a riverboat trip downstate, a lake resort up north in summer, a harness racing track in farm country, a high-stakes game in a hunting lodge.

**Excursions (settled 2026-10-16):** four trips at launch, one per season, each with a game you can play round after round. Every trip except Blackspruce Lodge can be repeated. Unlocks and seasons are starting values. Dropped: a second riverboat for a downriver run (it repeated the Prairie Queen), replaced by the train. The ice shanty camp on the frozen lake (poker in shanties, side bets on tip-ups, a Backwater crowd) is kept as a later add-on.
