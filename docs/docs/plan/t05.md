**Goal:** design how the player arrives and the first stretch of play.

**Already decided:** a blank-slate protagonist; the player is the protagonist. You arrive with about $200 and no dictated backstory. Choices build a reputation (hidden traits such as generous, reliable, ruthless) rather than a personality, and dialogue choices are action-first. Reason for coming (2026-10-16): never asked on a form; on the first night the motel clerk asks what brings you to Northlight, and you answer with an action (slide a twenty across, shrug, ask about the casino) or say it's none of their business. The answer sets a hidden trait and maybe a seed. Name (Mike, 2026-10-23): checking in, you sign Dot's register, which is where the player enters their name; the game uses it from then on. Arrival (2026-10-16): you drive in at night in a modest car (not necessarily beat-up), coming in through Backwater to the weekly motel; from the motel lot you see the Starflare's rocket sign across the river, the game's first image. The car matters later: going broke means sleeping in it under the Bridges. Considered and passed on: a reason picked from a menu at the start, arriving by bus or by train. First session (2026-10-16): bottom up. The first night stays small: the clerk's question, then you can't sleep and walk to Kasia's Tap for pull tabs and video poker at about a dollar a go, which teaches the core loop on your $200; a first seed lands (a stranger asks you to hold something). Kasia doesn't send you to the Flare; she'd rather keep you for her back room (Mike, 2026-10-23). Instead she offers you a couple of jobs, and taking the league's drink coupons to Juan at Millstone Lanes is where Juan tells you about the Starflare across the river. Then you cross the Long Bridge, stop at Bridgehead, and reach the Starflare. The opening is player-paced: Kasia's errands and the wedding take as long as you like, and the rocket sign across the river keeps the Flare in view ("three rungs in one session" dropped, audit 1.4, Mike 2026-10-24). Gus (2026-10-16): meet him early, care slowly. You meet Gus on your first visit to the Flare (a coffee, the regulars' trick of rubbing the spaceman's boot); caring builds over the first week from small things such as half the floor lights out or Gus asking you to keep quiet about something you saw, with no crisis yet. Considered and passed on: going straight to the Flare on night one, meeting Gus the first night, meeting him only once you're a regular. Reputation traits (2026-10-16): four hidden scales between opposites, each nudged by choices: generous or tight (tipping, buying rounds, covering debts, or stiffing people); reliable or flaky (keeping promises, paying debts on time, or skipping out); ruthless or soft (pressing a broke player, calling in debts, turning someone in, or letting it slide); straight or crooked (cheating, hustles, Umbra jobs, or playing clean; separate from heat, which is who's watching you). Numbers are never shown; you feel them through NPC reactions, events opening or closing, and things like how much credit Kasia gives you. Considered and passed on: 8–10 one-way tags; a reputation for luck was offered as a fifth scale and not taken. The Backwater motel is the Slack Water Motel (named 2026-10-17 in task 6). Gender (Mike, 2026-10-17, in task 6): the player picks their character's gender at the start, and the rival newcomer matches it. How (Mike, 2026-10-23): it's set at check-in, on Dot's register card, in a box beside the name with Mr., Ms. and Prefer not to answer (Mike, 2026-10-27). It also sets which wedding dance is offered first. For Prefer not to answer the rival is Chase or Brooke, picked at random (Mike, 2026-10-28). Honorifics follow the box: an NPC who says "sir" says "ma'am" for Ms. and uses none for Prefer not to answer (2026-10-28).
