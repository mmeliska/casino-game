**Goal:** give the player ways to earn money outside gambling that take time and shape who they become, and a phone that holds jobs, money and items.

**Already decided (Mike, 2026-10-19):** jobs are small, earn money and take time to complete. They show on a Jobs menu on the phone, which lists every job available right now; no corkboard. The phone is also where you manage money.

**Settled (Mike, 2026-10-24):**

- Pay: jobs pay less than a good night at the tables, so gambling stays the way up. Honest jobs pay $40 to $150 a shift; trusted repeat work for someone who trusts you pays up to about $250; crooked jobs pay $200 to $400 and each adds heat or another risk.
- How a shift plays: most shifts pay out in one panel with a line of flavor. The first shift, and about one in three after, is a short event with one or two choices from that job's own pool.
- Each job costs a slice of the day (morning, afternoon or night) or runs a few days, so its real cost is a night away from the tables; some carry a small deadline ("done by Friday"), never a story clock.
- The choices in a job's events nudge the hidden scales; the jobs you keep taking point you down a path (honest work toward the Flare, Rosa and the unions; Ray's work toward Umbra; Ed's toward informant).
- New employers start in person; after you meet them, their jobs appear in the Jobs tab on their own. Shady jobs arrive as texts from unknown numbers. The list refreshes daily; some jobs show only when you're broke or once someone trusts you.
- Repeat jobs for one person open their story.
- Phone tabs: Wallet (cash, Ray's loan and due date, Kasia's ledger, Flare markers (your own debts at the Flare's cage, not Gus's markers; Mike 2026-10-29), what Jalen owes (his tuition loan, repaid in tip envelopes; `suggestions/S-028`)), Jobs, Messages, Pockets (inventory). The phone can't place bets (online betting is illegal in-state).
- Inventory (Pockets): 12 slots for items you buy, sell, find or are given, every item meaningful (a suit for the Obsidian door, a gift to patch up Dot and Gus, tires, Gus's lucky chip: a last-night keepsake he presses into your hand at A3.3; after the closing it can't be cashed anywhere, and Troy buys it for almost nothing, Mike 2026-10-30). Story keys (Raffy's envelope and thumb drive; no room 9 key, which Richard keeps, Mike 2026-10-30) sit in their own row outside the 12. The row is "what they gave you" (`suggestions/S-009`, Mike 2026-10-28): it also holds Gus's IOU (S6), Pete's pin, the Cinder Room password Ray slipped you and Vivian's jeweler's receipt (if you kept quiet). Tapping a key opens a short dialog saying what it is and who gave it to you; an act 4 option that spends one names the giver; the record card lists what you kept. Richard gives no deck and has no key (Mike, 2026-10-29). Story keys can't be stolen (`suggestions/S-031`); the envelope's $2,000 isn't yours and doesn't count toward best bankroll until you choose to keep it at A1.5 (audit 2.10; A1.5 has four cash choices: return it to Ray, help Raffy pay Ray back by covering the missing $500, keep it, or keep it and tell Raffy it's settled; the drive stays with you, and its fate is chosen from act 2 on; `suggestions/S-050`, `S-058`) and can't be pawned unless a scene offers it.
- The car can't be sold. It is always your floor: rock bottom is sleeping in it under the Bridges.
- Crooked jobs form a ladder: bet running for Mitch, steering marks to the Cinder Room and selling information open early; collecting for Ray opens only after several Ray jobs, and can put someone you know (even Gus) in front of you. Their four rows in the table below are proposals (pay, moment, leads to).
- Pawn shop: Troy runs Bridgehead Loan & Pawn in the Bridges, next to Bridgehead Fuel & Food, between where you sleep and where you gamble. Buzzer door, cage counter, open until 3am. He pays a fraction and holds an item a few days so you can buy it back; some items are worth far more to one person. Look: male, white, stocky but strong, about 5'9", tattoos on his arms. Voice in the task 6 voice table. He is a functional NPC with a portrait and no story, on purpose (Mike, 2026-10-30).
