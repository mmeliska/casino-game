**Goal:** list every place you can gamble, with its theme, district and personality.

**Already decided:** the venue ladder from the game design document maps onto the districts, and every casino has a theme. Known so far: back-room games and street dice on the dry side, gas station slots at the main bridge, old riverboat casinos and the Starflare Club (rocket-age theme; the lakefront rebuild is simply the Starflare) in the river district, hotel casinos on the lakefront strip, and resorts, private clubs and yacht games on the gold coast. Roster (2026-10-16), poorest to richest: Backwater: street dice in the back lot of Millstone Lanes, a worn but still-open bowling alley where the mill shifts used to bowl, owned by Juan, who bought it from the old owner on a handshake payment plan about ten years ago (Mike, 2026-10-30), with league nights, a snack bar and a jukebox (renamed 2026-10-17 from the Suds-O-Mat laundromat, which was too close to Young Sheldon; the dice are fair in normal play, loaded only in an opt-in story; robberies and police sweeps) and the back room at Kasia's Tap (a dive bar card game the owner runs on credit). The Bridges: Bridgehead Fuel & Food at the foot of the Long Bridge (a few slots and a lottery counter; worst odds, best gossip). The Levee: the Starflare Club (faded rocket-age home base); the Prairie Queen (old paddlewheel riverboat built to haul prairie grain, still sails out before it deals; cheap tables, one crooked dealer); the Cinder Room (Umbra's after-hours game in the basement of a dead mill under the Stacks, roughly 2am to dawn; heat gets you in, not money; cash only, high stakes; a likely source of Starflare dirt and the mob-takeover ending). The Marquee Mile: the Harborlight (lighthouse-themed hotel casino, the local favorite) and the Aurelian (glass-tower chain casino from out of town, the Flare's real rival). Silvershore: the Obsidian (members-only card room; a suit and a sponsor) and yacht games (invite only, on whoever's boat is hosting). The lakefront Starflare appears only as an ending. Names dropped for clashes: Ida May (replaced), the Furnace, the Pembroke Club, Eclipse.

**Venue table (settled 2026-10-16):** bet ranges and milestones are starting values to tune in playtesting. Bankroll milestones count once reached, so a bad night doesn't lock you out. Rule (Mike, 2026-10-16): every game must be playable continuously, round after round, and win or lose money; one-off cons like three-card monte, the shell game or a bookie are events, not games. Story-event games (Mike, 2026-10-22): a story event can include a game played a set number of hands (for example a one-pass game of War with Richard: 10 cards each, every card flipped once, most cards wins); venue games stay replayable as much as the player wants. Cheating rule (Mike, revised 2026-10-19; replaces the 2026-10-17 opt-in version): ordinary play is always fair, so a losing player is never being cheated by the game. Doubt only comes up in story moments: a tip from a regular, a look from Rosa, a payout that seems short. In those moments the player can't be sure the table is crooked. Some tips are wrong and the dealer is just having a bad night, so the player has to read the clues and make the call. Every call has a consequence: accuse a crooked dealer and Umbra notices you; accuse an honest one and you lose standing at that venue and the regulars talk; ride a crooked table and you bleed money (crooked); walk and you never know at the time. The truth always surfaces later (a news item, a fired dealer, Rosa telling you), so the call lands like a bet won or lost. Inside a crooked table the odds shift for that session and the recent-results strip shows the pattern; Rosa's lessons sharpen the tells but never make you certain. The core five are slots, blackjack, craps, roulette and poker. Slots (Mike, 2026-10-19): every venue except the yacht games has a few versions of slots, themed to the place (proposed themes: bowling at Millstone Lanes, old reel machines at Kasia's, cheap three-reels at Bridgehead, rocket reels at the Flare, paddlewheels on the Prairie Queen, old one-armed bandits in the Cinder Room, lighthouse slots at the Harborlight, big-screen video and progressive jackpots at the Aurelian, a small high-limit slot salon at the Obsidian); at Kasia's they're "for amusement only" machines paid out by hand, and at Millstone Lanes the machines are free play, just for fun: a practice spot to learn them with no money (Mike, 2026-10-19; an exception to the play-for-money rule). Video poker (Mike, 2026-10-19) is in most venues: Millstone Lanes (free play), Kasia's, Bridgehead, the Flare, the Prairie Queen, the Harborlight, the Aurelian and the Obsidian; not the Cinder Room or the yacht games. Extra games beyond the core five: lottery scratchers, pull tabs, video poker, keno, the Rocket Wheel (a money wheel), and baccarat. Hazard was dropped as the Cinder Room's house game (Mike, 2026-10-19: too unfamiliar); seven-card stud is a poker variant at the Obsidian.
