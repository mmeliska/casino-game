## About

A small harness racing track, friendly to a broke player. You bet on races off Mitch's tips, some good and some not. Repeatable.

| Season | Games | Unlock | Who you'll find |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Late summer to fall | Race betting | A tip at Bridgehead | Mitch's world |

## Look and mood

Country daylight: a wooden grandstand, dust and hay.
