## About

Vivian's lakefront land: the site the lakefront Starflare needs; the Aurelian wants it too (A5.1). It's on the Marquee Mile, right at the Silvershore line: licensed and on the lake (audit 2.5, Mike 2026-10-26). The new rocket sign is relit here (A5.4).

## Look and mood

Between neon and old money: the Mile's glow on one side, Silvershore's dark shore on the other.

## Plate prompts

For Leonardo.ai or Adobe Firefly. A plate is a background with no people; an insert is a close-up of one of the place's props. The how-to, the negative prompts and the settings are on [Plate prompts](#plates). Each tag is also the image's file name and the scene's `# plate:` or `# insert:` tag.

**Proposed, not settled:** the lot.
