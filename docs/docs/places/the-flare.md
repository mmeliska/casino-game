## About

Gus's rocket-age casino, the Starflare Club, called "the Flare": once the best in the city, now slowly fading. Gus's father, Mr. Hamilton, founded it.

| District | Games | Bets | Getting in | Who you'll find |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| The Levee | Rocket-themed slots, video poker, blackjack, craps, roulette, poker, keno, the Rocket Wheel | $5–$100 | Anyone | Gus, Pete, Jalen, Stella, the regulars (Walt, Lorraine, Augie), Miles/Audrey in the lounge, Ray at the bar (from A1.5 on) |

**Gus's office.** Where Gus's secrets live: the tape of Rosa's table and the framed photo of Dot and her sister Alice.

**The lounge.** A half-empty piano lounge; Miles or Audrey plays nightly and overhears everything. The piano is covered as the Flare declines. Alice picked the piano and repainted the lounge's star ceiling with one constellation wrong on purpose.

**The rocket sign** stays on the empty Flare until demolition, then goes into the District's storage until the District restores it and hangs it over the Starflare Field gate as a heritage piece. It's lost if the Flare burns first (O9). In outcome 5 Tae takes it from storage or the gate (Mike, 2026-10-29). The new Starflare always gets a brand-new sign.

**The spaceman.** A life-size chrome astronaut statue by the front doors, waving. Regulars rub his boot toe for luck, worn shiny. If you helped the trucker (S3), he's saved on the last night and stands in the new Starflare's lobby.

**The burned shell.** If Umbra torches the empty Flare before demolition (O9), it's a setback you play through, not an ending: the Field is built on the burned site on its normal schedule (Mike, 2026-10-29).

## Look and mood

The Levee's key light is this sign, flickering. Teal, coral, chrome and rust; the Stacks outside every window. **Let decline show:** the floor plate gets darker over time (more lights out, a roped-off table, the bucket under the roof leak, the piano covered) without anyone saying so. Each stage is its own plate, generated from the first so it stays the same room.

## Plate prompts
