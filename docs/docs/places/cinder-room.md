## About

Umbra's after-hours game in an old mill basement under the Stacks; the password changes. Ray runs it, and the back office holds the enrollment station where a man pays to be unseen by Revaris's cameras. Wren appears only once, in the office (A4.5), where she turns the cameras off before she speaks.

## Look and mood

Under the Stacks: brick, iron and ember light. The Levee palette goes to rust here. Ray's burn bag is in the office closet.
