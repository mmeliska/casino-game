## About

A lighthouse-themed hotel casino; a friendly first rung up. Your first comped suite comes with it (A2.4). Bennett launches his mayoral run at a fundraiser here (A2.6).

## Look and mood

Hotel marquees and LED walls; magenta, gold, lake blue. A nautical theme: white and navy, a lighthouse lens over the floor.
