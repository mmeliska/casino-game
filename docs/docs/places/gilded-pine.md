## About

An old-money lake resort up north, open in every season (winter pines make good scenery; Mike, 2026-10-30). Repeatable. You meet Vivian here properly once you reach $25,000 (A3.6).

| Season | Games | Unlock | Who you'll find |

## Look and mood

Silvershore's look out of town: lamplight, ivory, deep green, brass; tall pines and a still lake. Snow is a layer for winter.

## Plate prompts

For Leonardo.ai or Adobe Firefly. A plate is a background with no people; an insert is a close-up of one of the place's props. The how-to, the negative prompts and the settings are on [Plate prompts](#plates). Each tag is also the image's file name and the scene's `# plate:` or `# insert:` tag.
