## About

Old downtown on the river: the Starflare, the riverboats, the Stacks. **The Stacks** are the dead mill chimneys, outside every Levee window. The union rally of A3.2 happens here.

## Look and mood

The rocket sign flickering; teal, coral, chrome, rust; river mist.

## Plate prompts

For Leonardo.ai or Adobe Firefly. A plate is a background with no people; an insert is a close-up of one of the place's props. The how-to, the negative prompts and the settings are on [Plate prompts](#plates). Each tag is also the image's file name and the scene's `# plate:` or `# insert:` tag.

**Proposed, not settled:** the rally stage on a flatbed.
