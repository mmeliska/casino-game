## About

The futuristic rebuild on Vivian's land, owned and licensed by you, with Jalen and Vivian as minority partners. Its opening night ends the main story (A5.6). The tower and rooftop launch pad come after. It always gets a brand-new rocket sign; if you saved the spaceman (S3), he stands in its lobby. It runs its own cameras, never Revaris's.

## Look and mood

Rocket-age heritage made new: the Flare's teal and coral against the Mile's lake blue and gold.

## Plate prompts

For Leonardo.ai or Adobe Firefly. A plate is a background with no people; an insert is a close-up of one of the place's props. The how-to, the negative prompts and the settings are on [Plate prompts](#plates). Each tag is also the image's file name and the scene's `# plate:` or `# insert:` tag.

**Proposed, not settled:** the building and the floor; the new sign's design (you pick it at A5.4).
