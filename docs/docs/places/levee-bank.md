## About

The old Levee bank holds Gus's safety deposit box: Wren's camera contract and Gus's own recording of Bennett's demand, the only copy. Richard wears the key. You open it together when Umbra comes after Richard over the debt (A4.4). The bank isn't named yet.

## Look and mood

Old money in an old district: marble, brass and quiet.

## Plate prompts

For Leonardo.ai or Adobe Firefly. A plate is a background with no people; an insert is a close-up of one of the place's props. The how-to, the negative prompts and the settings are on [Plate prompts](#plates). Each tag is also the image's file name and the scene's `# plate:` or `# insert:` tag.

**Proposed, not settled:** everything (only its purpose is settled).
