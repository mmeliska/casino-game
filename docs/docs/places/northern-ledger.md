## About

An overnight train to the state capital during the legislative session, with poker in the club car. Repeatable.

| Season | Games | Unlock | Who you'll find |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Spring | Club-car poker | Levee alderman's invite or $10,000 once | The Machine, Bennett, Mayor Dabareski, Graham/Claire |

## Look and mood

Wood panelling, brass and lamplight moving through a spring night.
