## About

Bridgehead Loan & Pawn, Troy's shop next to Bridgehead Fuel & Food, between where you sleep and where you gamble (Mike, 2026-10-24). Buzzer door, cage counter, open until 3am. He buys your items for a fraction and holds each one a few days so you can buy it back. If the rival steals from you (A4.8), the item turns up behind his glass with the rival's watch beside it.

## Look and mood

The Bridges' flat white light, behind security bars.

## Plate prompts

For Leonardo.ai or Adobe Firefly. A plate is a background with no people; an insert is a close-up of one of the place's props. The how-to, the negative prompts and the settings are on [Plate prompts](#plates). Each tag is also the image's file name and the scene's `# plate:` or `# insert:` tag.
