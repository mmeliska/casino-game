## About

The hearing room, used twice: the council hearing in the fight (A3.2) and the state gaming commission's license hearing (A5.7), which reuses it. The commission also hears the conflict-of-interest complaint (A4.11) and the short hearings in A5.5.

## Look and mood

Civic, not neon: wood, flat daylight.

## Plate prompts

For Leonardo.ai or Adobe Firefly. A plate is a background with no people; an insert is a close-up of one of the place's props. The how-to, the negative prompts and the settings are on [Plate prompts](#plates). Each tag is also the image's file name and the scene's `# plate:` or `# insert:` tag.

**Proposed, not settled:** the room.
