## About

Juan's struggling bowling alley. He bought it from the old owner on a handshake payment plan (Mike, 2026-10-30), keeps the ancient pinsetters running with spare parts and stubbornness, and runs dice out back after the last frame; the dice money pays for the parts. Inside, the machines are free play, just for fun: the place to practise. It refuses Revaris cameras (Juan's dice and his regulars). Ed talks straight here.

| District | Games | Bets | Getting in | Who you'll find |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Backwater | Craps in the back lot (real money); inside, bowling-themed slots and video poker on free play, just for fun | $1–$20 | Anyone; sweeps likelier when your heat is high | Juan, league regulars; Raffy hiding out later |

## Look and mood

The bowling-alley sign is one of Backwater's key lights. Inside: league night, worn lanes, old pinsetters. Out back: asphalt, a bare bulb, dice. If Umbra's push succeeds (R3), lane six goes dark; if a raid is traced to you, a sign about the pinsetters appears in the window.
