## About

Bennett's domed baseball stadium on the Flare's site, with the old rocket sign restored over its gate. Its timer starts at the ground-breaking (A3.5); if it opens before the name is won back, the name stays with the Field (O7).

## Look and mood

A new civic landmark in the Levee: concrete, glass and the old coral sign.

## Plate prompts

For Leonardo.ai or Adobe Firefly. A plate is a background with no people; an insert is a close-up of one of the place's props. The how-to, the negative prompts and the settings are on [Plate prompts](#plates). Each tag is also the image's file name and the scene's `# plate:` or `# insert:` tag.
