## Look and mood

Yachts on a dark lake, the city glowing far off. The deck plate keeps the motel lot's framing, the city across the water in the upper left, but what's across the water now is the lakefront skyline. The Flare is inland on the river and can't be seen from the lake, so there's no rocket sign (Mike, 2026-10-31).

## Plate prompts

For Leonardo.ai or Adobe Firefly. A plate is a background with no people; an insert is a close-up of one of the place's props. The how-to, the negative prompts and the settings are on [Plate prompts](#plates). Each tag is also the image's file name and the scene's `# plate:` or `# insert:` tag.

**Proposed, not settled:** the yacht.
