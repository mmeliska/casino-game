## About

One-time, invite-only high-stakes poker at a remote lodge in winter. Ray needs a stake partner and asks you along (X4).

| Season | Games | Unlock | Who you'll find |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Winter | Poker | Ray's winter invitation once you're a Cinder Room regular, plus a best bankroll of about $25,000 (a starting value; Mike, 2026-10-30) | Umbra, old money, Ray |

## Look and mood

Dark logs, firelight and snow. Snow is baked in.

## Plate prompts

For Leonardo.ai or Adobe Firefly. A plate is a background with no people; an insert is a close-up of one of the place's props. The how-to, the negative prompts and the settings are on [Plate prompts](#plates). Each tag is also the image's file name and the scene's `# plate:` or `# insert:` tag.
