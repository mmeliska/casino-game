## About

The first step up the housing ladder: a room over a bar in the Levee. The bar isn't named yet.

## Look and mood

Neon from the bar below through the window; the Stacks and the rocket sign close.

## Plate prompts

For Leonardo.ai or Adobe Firefly. A plate is a background with no people; an insert is a close-up of one of the place's props. The how-to, the negative prompts and the settings are on [Plate prompts](#plates). Each tag is also the image's file name and the scene's `# plate:` or `# insert:` tag.

**Proposed, not settled:** everything.

### Your room at night

Going to sleep; the phone at night. Shows the rocket sign: from the ground-breaking (A3.5) on, when the Flare is demolished, make a variant without it.

`levee_room_night` (plate, portrait 9:16)

```
Background plate, no people. A small rented room over a bar: an iron bed, a dresser, a radiator, a window full of teal and coral neon from the street, old mill chimneys and a coral rocket sign outside in the upper left. Night: teal and coral neon, chrome highlights. Palette: teal, coral, chrome, rust. Gritty graphic-novel background art: bold black ink outlines, heavy shadows, cel shading, muted desaturated colours, realistic perspective. Eye-level view from standing height, open floor in the lower middle of the frame. No people, no text, no lettering; signs blank.
```
