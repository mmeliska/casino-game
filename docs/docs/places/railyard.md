## About

A locals' casino in the old rail depot on the Levee's edge: clean, busy, a little plain, with a buffet; what the Flare could be if Gus ran it well. The mill pensioners love it, the rival shows off here early (A1.8), and most of the Flare's regulars land here after the fall (A3.5). Its face-scan players' card is seed S10.

| District | Games | Bets | Getting in | Who you'll find |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| The Levee, the old rail depot at its edge | Rail-themed slots, video poker, blackjack, craps, roulette, poker, keno; players' card and weeknight slot tournaments | $10–$500 | Reach $1,500 once | Mill pensioners, Chase/Brooke early on; most Flare regulars after the fall |

Mike named it on 2026-10-19; the details are proposals. No casino or well-known game uses the name; Santa Fe and Texarkana have "Railyard" districts, but it's a common word. It fills the gap between the Flare ($5 to $100) and the Harborlight ($25 to $1,000).

## Look and mood

Bright and tidy compared with the Flare: the depot's arched hall, iron trusses and a big station clock, lit evenly.
