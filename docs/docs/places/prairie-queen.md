## About

An old-time riverboat; you're aboard until it docks. Kiran deals table four, and the skim (M3) runs through it.

| District | Games | Bets | Getting in | Who you'll find |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| The Levee | Blackjack, craps, roulette, paddlewheel slots, video poker | $5–$200 | A ticket for a set sailing | Kiran; Rosa near the landing |

**The landing.** Rosa's hangout near the boat: paddle lights, river mist.

**The sister boat.** An old pilot grumbles about the Queen's sister boat rusting at a south pier (S5). If the land falls through, it's there and cheap: the Starflare floats (O3).

## Look and mood

River mist and the paddle lights are the Levee's weather layer, baked into the deck and landing plates. The Stacks and the rocket sign are on the shore. Use the setting as pressure: you can't leave until it docks.
