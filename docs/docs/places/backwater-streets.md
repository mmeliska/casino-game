## About

The streets of Backwater: mill families, back rooms, street dice. Walking between Kasia's, the Lanes and the motel; a place for random encounters and travel break points.

## Look and mood

Sodium streetlight, bar neon, steam from mill grates, rain on asphalt by night; warm and busy by day.

## Plate prompts

For Leonardo.ai or Adobe Firefly. A plate is a background with no people; an insert is a close-up of one of the place's props. The how-to, the negative prompts and the settings are on [Plate prompts](#plates). Each tag is also the image's file name and the scene's `# plate:` or `# insert:` tag.
