## About

A Levee union hall: where the regulars can pool small shares and go co-op, if the name is won back while you're short of $250,000 (A4.10) or the land falls through (A5.5). The people's Starflare (O4) runs co-op nights here (licensed after a short commission hearing; Mike, 2026-10-30).

## Look and mood

Old, worn and warm.

## Plate prompts

For Leonardo.ai or Adobe Firefly. A plate is a background with no people; an insert is a close-up of one of the place's props. The how-to, the negative prompts and the settings are on [Plate prompts](#plates). Each tag is also the image's file name and the scene's `# plate:` or `# insert:` tag.

**Proposed, not settled:** everything.
