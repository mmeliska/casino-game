## About

A members-only card room in Silvershore. Vivian sponsors you (A3.6). A society columnist asks how much Vivian lost tonight (S9).

| District | Games | Bets | Getting in | Who you'll find |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Silvershore | Blackjack (high-roller side bets), poker (including seven-card stud), roulette, craps, a small high-limit salon of slots and video poker, baccarat | $500 and up | Reach $50,000 once; a suit; a member sponsors you; low heat | Vivian |

## Look and mood

Warm lamps and candlelight; ivory, deep green, brass. No neon is the luxury.
