## Summary
Give Rosa a want and a need beyond her license: she wants to be good at the only thing she was ever good at, in public; she needs to stop protecting Gus. Her arc's end is a threshold: she walks into a Starflare. And Gus, who has not said her name since the payroll night, says it.

## Current state
Rosa "really was guilty, but for a good reason"; "took the fall without saying why"; "never mentions the Starflare"; "was never a Flare regular and won't step inside it" (people/rosa.md). Payoff: her license, won back by solving M1; a job wherever you run a floor. Gus "kept the only wide-angle tape" and "admits it" when M1 is solved (plan/t08.md). No page says whether Gus and Rosa have spoken since, or what Rosa wants beyond the license.

## Proposed change
**Want:** to deal again, licensed, where people can see her hands. Kasia's back room is dealing in the dark; she's precise there because precision is all she has left.

**Need:** to stop paying for Gus. She has made his silence her rule ("never mentions the Starflare"), and the rule is a cell with the door open.

**Contradiction:** her lessons are confessions (S-023). She teaches the player to read the hand that isn't busy because she was the hand that wasn't busy.

**Turning point:** the hearing (A5.7). If she is one of your witnesses, she speaks for you and not about herself; if you take the concealment onto your own record (S-001, "Take it"), she is the one person who knows what you did. (S-002's version, where Gus said the debt was his and her own rule broke in the hearing room, went with S-002's rejection on 2026-10-27; her "never mentions the Starflare" rule now holds all game, and the door is where it ends.)

**End:** opening night (O1, O2) or the first night of a floor you run (O3, O4). Rosa stops at the door. Pose: one finger on the frame, the same gesture as on the felt. Then in. Gus, at the first table (a retired guest in O1 and O2, S-001; the regulars' host in O4): "Rosa." Nothing else. (He has not said it in years; see S-014.) In O2 she still comes to deal, and the name is said by Pete.

**Badly:** if the player rides a crooked table she flagged or pushes her into the Flare before the license, she stops teaching. Every later Rosa scene is rules only, no reads, and her payoff still fires through M1 without her ever walking onto your floor; she deals at the Harborlight, as the docs already allow, and the Harborlight's dealer at your table one night is her, and she doesn't look up.

## Why it works
"Won't step inside" is the best constraint in her sheet and the docs never spend it. A threshold crossed once is the panel kit's cheapest arc. Giving Gus a single word to say at the door pays off two rules (his and hers) in one panel, and the bad ending uses a venue the docs already send her to.

## How the player experiences it
The doorway panel; Gus's one word; or, badly, a dealer at the Harborlight who doesn't look up.

## Risks and tradeoffs
Costs nothing in art. The hearing variant depends on S-001, but the door scene stands alone. Don't give Rosa a speech at the threshold; the finger on the frame is the line.
