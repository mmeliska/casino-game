## Summary
Name the five moments the story should be built around, where each lands in the pacing, and what it needs from the player. The acts already have gates and plot beats; they don't yet have a stated emotional beat each.

## Current state
The spine is five acts with money and time gates (pages/storymap-intro.md). Each act has a plot function (attachment, bailout, fall, name, rebuild). No page names the feeling each act is for or the single scene that should carry it.

## Proposed change
| Act | Beat | Feeling | The scene that carries it | Needs |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | **Pete says your name** | Belonging | The night Pete's rating card shows a rating, he drops "kid" and uses the name you wrote on Dot's register (A1.7) | S-019 |
| 2 | **The photo of the sisters** | The Flare is a mausoleum | During A2.1 in Gus's office, the insert of the two sisters, no caption; later Dot says her sister's name and you can say it to Gus | S-013 |
| 3 | **The last night** | Loss | Three slots, six scenes, and the ones you didn't choose (A3.3) | S-003 |
| 4 | **Gus goes over the water** | Confession | Gus at Dot's picnic table admitting he sold his dad's place to the people who blackmailed him (A4.1) | S-004 |
| 5 | **The hard question** | What you'll carry for someone | The hearing (A5.7), then the ribbon | S-001 or S-002 |

Pacing: beats 1 and 2 arrive before the player has spent much money, so they are cheap to reach; 3 is the midpoint and "always happens"; 4 and 5 are gated by money, so the player earns them. Every other scene in the game should be able to answer "which of the five is this setting up or paying off?"

## Why it works
A long, open life sim needs a short list of moments that the writing serves, or the beats blur into gates. These five are belonging, mourning, loss, confession and sacrifice, which is the shape of a story about a man's father's house. They are also all cheap in art and all already half-present in the docs.

## How the player experiences it
As five scenes that feel different from the rest: each gets a black panel, a silence, and a stake. The epilogue's cast panels can quote one line from each beat the player reached.

## Risks and tradeoffs
Naming beats risks over-signposting if the writing winks at them. Keep them unmarked. The table depends on several other suggestions, and beat 5 needs one of S-001 or S-002 (they conflict, so neither is listed as a dependency); accepting the list without them still gives the writing a target.
