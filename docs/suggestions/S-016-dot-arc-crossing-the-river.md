## Summary
Give Dot a want, a need and a turn: she wants everyone safe on her side of the river; she needs to go to the Levee once more, for her sister's husband. Her arc ends when she crosses the Long Bridge, which she hasn't done since the funeral.

## Current state
Dot owns the Slack Water, works the desk, warns you sideways, tells stories, "never talks about Gus," and hasn't spoken to him since the funeral (people/dot.md). She appears in A1.1, A4.2 ("help him quit, with Dot and Stella") and the reconciliation thread. There is no stated reason she stays in Backwater or any scene of her leaving it.

## Proposed change
**Want:** to keep her people on the dry side, where the only thing that can hurt them is weather. Every warning she gives is this want talking.

**Wound:** two hours of ringing a phone (S-012). Then Gus refused her the one thing she asked for, their mother's recipe box (S-011), and the silence became a break. She let her sister marry into the Levee and believes, without ever saying it, that the Levee took her.

**Contradiction:** the warmest person in the game takes an envelope from a driver every week for a man whose story she has never asked, and warns everyone off the Cinder Room without knowing the same outfit watches her own lot. She tells herself room 9 is none of her business, which is the same thing Gus tells himself about the Cinder Room.

**Turn:** A4.2 ends at her lot (Mike, 2026-10-28). You get Gus out of the Cinder Room and drive him over the Long Bridge before dawn. Dot walks out to the car and says one thing to him, S-012's "Two ten," the first words she has said to him since the funeral. She has not crossed the river since the funeral; make that a rule. Her desk is not a rule: she can come out from behind it whenever a scene needs her to.

**End:** opening night, she crosses the Long Bridge. In O1 she goes to find the ceiling (S-013). In O6 (Gus lets go) she doesn't need to cross: he comes to her, and the resting point's "visits with sober Gus, Dot and Stella" happen at the picnic table. In O2, she comes anyway and leaves before the ribbon.

**Badly:** if the player sells Gus out (O10), Dot takes your name out of the register. Literally: a panel of her tearing the page. You can still rent a room; you're a guest now.

## Why it works
Dot is the game's first voice and the player's first kindness, which makes her the best person to carry the cost of Gus's secret without a speech. A woman who hasn't crossed the river since the funeral, then does, is the cheapest arc the panel kit can draw. The torn register page is the harshest consequence in the game that costs no money, which is what the sellout needs.

## How the player experiences it
Her first words to Gus at dawn on her lot. Her crossing on opening night. The torn page if you earned it.

## Risks and tradeoffs
The river rule constrains her: every Dot scene before opening night is in Backwater. S-012 carries most of the weight; without it her turn is still a reconciliation, just a lighter one.

## Decision notes
- Mike, 2026-10-28: accepted with a change: no behind-the-desk rule. A4.2 ends at Dot's lot: you drive Gus over and she speaks to him there. Her rule is only that she hasn't crossed the river since the funeral, until opening night.
