## Summary
Stage Vivian's arc on one rule: the sunglasses stay on indoors in every scene until one. The scene where she takes them off is the one where she sells the land, or decides not to.

## Current state
"Dark sunglasses indoors" (look); tell: "sunglasses pushed up; pearls in her fist" (people/vivian.md). Her beats: glimpsed at the Harborlight (A2.4), met at the Gilded Pine (A3.6), Obsidian sponsor, yacht invite, the drive job and S9 (silence lowers the land price), A5.1 (win her or help Tae). No scene is written for A5.1.

## Proposed change
**Start:** the glimpse at the Harborlight, sunglasses on, pearls, a stack she's losing without looking at it.

**Middle:** the Gilded Pine, the Obsidian, the yacht. The rule: sunglasses on in every indoor panel. The tell (pushed up) is the only movement. The drive job and the jeweler are where the player learns she's selling pieces; S9's columnist is where the player decides whether to say so.

**Turn, A5.1 (two versions):**
- *Win her:* the Obsidian's private room, late. She takes the sunglasses off and sets them on the felt, the first time the player sees her eyes. New pose, the only new Vivian art. "Darling, I've been losing since before you were born. I'm very good at it." Beat. "This one I'd like to win." She names the price (lower the closer you are; the friend's price if S-033's variant fires). The sunglasses stay off for the rest of the scene and go back on at the door.
- *Help Tae (sellout):* same room, Tae present, sunglasses on. She signs. She never takes them off again in any later scene, and the pearls-in-fist tell stops, because there's nothing left to tell.

**End:** opening night, O1 or O2, she's at the back of the room, sunglasses on, and when the sign lights she pushes them up and leaves them there. If the ceiling has Alice's constellation (S-013), she's looking at it.

**Badly:** she sells to Tae, the Aurelian tower goes up where the Starflare would be, and the player sees her once more at the Gilded Pine, a season later, at a lower-limit table, in sunglasses, winning, and not enjoying it.

## Why it works
One visual rule, broken once, is the panel kit's whole method. Eyes seen once in a game is a memory; the player will know the moment the scene starts. The sellout branch denying the player her eyes forever is a consequence with no number.

## How the player experiences it
The rule is never stated. The new pose at A5.1 is the reward for getting there.

## Risks and tradeoffs
One portrait pose. The "badly" scene needs her to appear post-sellout, which the Gilded Pine already allows.
