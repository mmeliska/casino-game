## Summary
Turn the camera dead zone into a writing rule: the scenes where someone tells the truth happen in Backwater or on the Long Bridge. Move Gus's confession (A4.1) to the Slack Water Motel, hold Ed's honest talks at Millstone Lanes, and give Wren one scene where she has to cross the river.

## Current state
Revaris cameras cover the city; dead zones are "all of Backwater" and the Long Bridge (pages/gdd.md, people/wren.md). The docs use this for logic: Raffy hides in Backwater, Ray meets the lawyer on the bridge, Umbra must watch room 9 in person. A4.1 "The truth" has no location (pages/storymap-beats.md). Ed's squad runs on the readers and "he knows it and hates it" (people/ed.md). Wren is "rarely seen" and A4.5 is at the Cinder Room, under her own cameras.

## Proposed change
A one-line rule for writers: **people say what they mean where nothing is recording.** Three applications:

- **A4.1, Gus crosses the river.** Gus has never been "over the water" in the game. He drives to the Slack Water Motel, sits at the picnic table, and tells you he sold his dad's place to the people who blackmailed him. Dot sees him from the desk and does not come out. The player's forgive-or-hold-it choice stays as written; A4.2 ("help him quit, with Dot and Stella") now lands on the same lot once you've got him out of the Cinder Room (S-015), which is where Dot already is.
- **Ed talks straight at the Lanes.** Every informant-path conversation with Ed happens at Millstone Lanes or Kasia's. His reason, once: "My own squad's readers. I don't talk business where my boss can pull the tape." The honest cop is honest only where his department can't watch him, which is the irony the docs already set up.
- **Wren, once, without her cameras.** A variant of A4.5: if the player has never scanned in (S10) and has stayed in Backwater for the act's key moves, Wren comes to the Slack Water lot in person. For the only time in the game she asks a question she doesn't know the answer to (S-042).

## Why it works
Geography becomes meaning. The cameras are currently a mechanism the player is told about; this makes them something the player feels, because confession, honesty and vulnerability all happen on the dry side, and the Levee's neon is where people perform. It also solves A4.1's missing location with the most loaded place in the game: Gus confessing in front of the sister-in-law he hasn't spoken to since the funeral.

## How the player experiences it
Nothing on screen names the rule. Gus's portrait over the Backwater motel plate is the tell; so is Ed's coffee on Juan's counter. A player who notices that every honest scene happens across the river has found a pattern the game never explains.

## Risks and tradeoffs
Costs almost nothing in art (existing portraits over existing plates). The risk is overuse: if every scene is "let's go to Backwater" the rule becomes a joke. Keep it to confessions and the three scenes above, and let the Levee keep its lies.
