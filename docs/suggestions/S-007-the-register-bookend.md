## Summary
The name the player writes on Dot's register on night one is the name they write again on the license at the hearing, and Dot brings the motel register to opening night so the first guest line in the new Starflare's book is written under the old one.

## Current state
A1.1 (pages/storymap-beats.md): you sign Dot's register with your name and tick Mr. or Ms.; "the game uses it from then on." A5.7 and A5.6 have no signing. The epilogue builder ends on "your record card" (plan/t10.md).

## Proposed change
- **At the hearing (A5.7)**, the last panel before the ruling is the same register-card insert from night one, now a license application, with the name field filled in the player's own handwriting (the same rendered string).
- **On opening night (A5.6)**, Dot arrives with the Slack Water's register under her arm. She opens it to the page from your first night and puts it on the new front desk as the guestbook. The first line is yours, in the handwriting of someone who had $200. The second is Dot's. In O2 (without Gus) she still comes, and the line under yours is empty for a panel before she signs. If she tore your page out once (S-016, the sellout) and you climbed back, she brings the book anyway, and the torn edge is the panel.
- **At a resting point or the drive-away**, the record card's first line is the register line, dated to day one.

## Why it works
The blank-slate protagonist has exactly one thing that is theirs from the first minute: the name they typed. Making the player see it again at the two moments that define the ending is ritual and bookend, and it gives Dot, who asked the game's only personal question, the last word on who you turned out to be. It costs one reused insert.

## How the player experiences it
Two inserts and one line from Dot. Nothing is explained; the player recognises the handwriting.

## Risks and tradeoffs
If the name is rendered as a font rather than a handwriting-style string, the bookend reads weaker; worth a dedicated "register" font. Dot bringing the register to opening night needs her to cross the river, which the docs have her do only for A4.2 at the Cinder Room (and S-004 moves that to her own lot); S-016 makes the crossing her arc's end, so the two should be taken together.
