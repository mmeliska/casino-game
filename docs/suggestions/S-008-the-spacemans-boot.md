## Summary
When Gus shows you the boot trick on your first visit (A1.6), he admits he has never done it himself. On opening night, before the ribbon, he does.

## Current state
A1.6: "Coffee with Gus, rub the spaceman's boot" (pages/storymap-beats.md). The spaceman is "a life-size chrome astronaut statue by the doors; regulars rub his boot toe for luck, worn shiny" (pages/northlight.md). S3's trucker saves it on the last night and it stands in the new lobby (plan/t09.md). Gus laughs at the lucky bobblehead (S12).

## Proposed change
A1.6, after he shows you the boot: "Everybody does it. I never did. Felt like asking for something." Pose: proud chin-up. That's the whole seed; no flag needed beyond the beat itself.

Payoffs:
- **O1, opening night.** The spaceman stands in the new lobby (if saved) or a new one does. Before the ribbon, Gus stops, looks at the boot, and rubs it. Insert: his hand on the chrome. No line. Then he cuts the ribbon.
- **O2, without Gus.** You cut the ribbon alone. The boot of the new spaceman is unworn and mirror-bright; nobody has touched it yet. Insert, no caption.
- **O4, the people's Starflare.** The regulars carry the old spaceman into the union hall and set him by the door; Gus, hosting, is the first to rub the boot, in front of everyone.
- **O9, burned down.** The spaceman's boot is the only part of him that didn't blacken.

## Why it works
A pride tell planted in the first ten minutes and paid off in the last. Gus "asking for something" is the arc of a proud man in one gesture, shown through a prop the player already touches every visit (technique: ritual plus vulnerability). It also gives the trucker seed a second reason to matter.

## How the player experiences it
One extra line on the first visit, one insert at each ending. The player who remembers the line gets the moment; the one who doesn't sees an old man touch a statue.

## Risks and tradeoffs
Near zero cost. The only risk is competing with S-002 for Gus's "pride breaks" moment at the end; they can coexist because one is public and one is private, but if both are taken, the boot should come second.
