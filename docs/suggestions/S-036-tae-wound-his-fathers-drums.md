## Summary
Give Tae one object and one buried fact: when the house band folded, his father's kit stayed in the Flare's basement because Gus's father wouldn't let him sell it, and it's still there. Tae knows. "World-class" is the word he uses for everything his father wasn't, and the Flare is the only place that ever treated his father as if he were.

## Current state
Tae grew up in the Levee; his dad played drums in the Flare's rocket-age house band; "he grew up hearing the Flare through the wall and has a soft spot for it he'd never admit"; rivalry of visions, not a villain; "doesn't lie at all, which is what makes him dangerous"; the band photo seed (S4) drops his guard; the house band reunion (Miles/Audrey's exclusive) softens the name's price; his restaurant hangs the band photo (O5) (people/tae.md, plan/t09.md, people/miles.md).

## Proposed change
**The fact.** Tae's father drummed at the Flare for eleven years and then for nobody; when the band folded he tried to sell the kit to cover a bad month and Mr. Hamilton bought it from him for more than it was worth and left it in the basement "for the next band." There never was one. The kit is still there, under a tarp, behind the one-armed bandits the Cinder Room didn't take. Tae's father died with a Flare paycheck stub in his wallet from the last week the band played. Tae has never been down to the basement. He has, in fact, asked Gus, once, years ago, if the kit was still there, and Gus said "Anyway. Coffee?"

**Contradiction:** a man who never lies has one subject he never raises. His rivalry is a bid to build the kind of house his father could have been proud to play in; he can't say that, so he says "Northlight deserves world-class," and means his father did.

**How it shows:**
- **S4 sharpened.** When you say the drummer's name at the Aurelian, Tae's guard drops as settled, and he asks one question: "Is the kit still there?" The player may not know. If they go and look (the Flare's basement, a plate), it is.
- **The last night (S-003 or A3.3).** The kit is one of the things that has to leave the building. The player can get it to Tae (a job: a flatbed, the trucker if S3 fired) or let the District's crew haul it with the rest. If Tae gets it, it sits in the Aurelian's loading dock for a season, in a tarp, because he doesn't know what to do with it either.
- **Payoff.** In O5 (Tae's restaurant), the kit is on a riser under the band photo. In O1, if the reunion fires, Tae's father's kit is on the showroom stage, and Tae is in the room, and he doesn't say anything, which from Tae is a speech.

## Why it works
The soft spot "he'd never admit" needs an object to be about, or it stays a note. A drum kit is cheap (one insert), it ties Tae to the Flare's physical fate at the moment it matters (the last night), and it gives the reunion route a prop to pay off. It also puts a second father's thing in the Flare's basement.

## How the player experiences it
One question from Tae; one basement plate; one optional job; one insert at the end.

## Risks and tradeoffs
Adds a small basement location and an item too big for Pockets (handle as a scene, not inventory). The Hamilton purchase gives the founder a second offstage kindness (with S-033); both are memories, no scenes.
