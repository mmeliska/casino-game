## Summary
Make Pete's "calls everyone kid until they earn their name" a gameplay moment: after a few sittings at the Flare, Pete uses your name, once, and it's the name from Dot's register. That is the act 1 emotional beat: you belong somewhere.

## Current state
Pete "calls everyone 'kid' until they earn their name" and holds "a rating card in hand" (people/pete.md); his sample line: "Kid. Your rating says you're a $50 player. Play like one." The name is entered at A1.1 and "the game uses it from then on" (pages/player.md). A1.7 "Becoming a regular" has the choice "keep Gus's secret or not" and leads to "meet Pete and Jalen." The review proposed Pete's card show "what the Flare thinks you're worth" (pages/review.md). Comps tied to the card are a build note, not a scene.

## Proposed change
- **No card on screen** (Mike, 2026-10-28). Pete's rating of you stays hidden: the Flare tracks your sittings (average bet, hours) and the player never sees it. Pete holds his rating card in scenes as the docs have it, a prop with no numbers shown.
- **The moment.** After a few sittings at the Flare (tuned), the next break point there fires a four-panel scene: Pete at the pit, card in hand, pose unchanged, and the line, with your register name in it: "[Name]. Your rating says you're a $50 player. Play like one." Nothing else changes. Comps start the next visit.
- **The rule afterward.** Pete uses your name from then on, and goes back to "kid" exactly once: the dark pit (A3.4), when he hands you the pin. If you sold Gus out, he never says your name again.
- **Jalen notices.** If Jalen is on the floor he says, politely, "He doesn't do that." That's the only comment.

## Why it works
Belonging is the first thing a blank-slate player needs and the hardest to give without a speech. The name is the one thing the player made; hearing it from the man who withholds names turns a settings field into a reward (technique: earned recognition, ritual). It also makes the comp system the leash the review asked for, because Pete's rating is the first thing Tae's Aurelian will "back off."

## How the player experiences it
One short scene, and a change in address that the player will hear in every Pete line afterward.

## Risks and tradeoffs
No number is shown, so the "scales are never shown as numbers" rule holds; the only number is the one Pete says out loud, which the docs already give him. The reversion to "kid" at the dark pit should be a pose-less, single-word choice by the writer; if it reads as cruelty, drop it and keep the name.

## Decision notes
- Mike, 2026-10-28: accepted without the card: no tappable rating card on screen. The name scene stays.
