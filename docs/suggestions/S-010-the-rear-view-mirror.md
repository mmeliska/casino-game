## Summary
The game's first image is the rocket sign across the river from the motel lot. The drive-away epilogue's first panel is the same composition in the rear-view mirror, with the sign in whatever state the story left it, and one line from the person you owe most.

## Current state
Arrival: "from the motel lot you see the Starflare's rocket sign across the river, the game's first image" (plan/t05.md). Drive-away: "a leaving epilogue built from that day ... framed differently (rear-view mirror versus ribbon cutting)" (plan/t10.md). The sign's state over time is settled: flickering, dark on the shell, in District storage, over the Field gate, burned, or new on the lakefront (pages/northlight.md).

## Proposed change
The leaving epilogue's panel 1 is the Long Bridge at night from the driver's seat, the mirror showing the sign in its current state:

| Where the story stands | The mirror shows |
| --- | --- |
| Act 2 | The sign flickering over the Flare |
| After the fall | The dark shell, sign unlit |
| After demolition | Empty sky over the Levee; the Stacks |
| Field open (O7) | The sign over a stadium gate |
| Burned (O9) | A black shell, no sign |
| O5 | The old sign lit inside the Aurelian's restaurant window |
| O3, O4 | No sign: the Prairie Queen's sister boat's paddle lights, or the union hall's door |

Panel 1's only text is a line from the person you owe the most (a debt in the Wallet, or the highest standing if you owe nothing): Kasia: "Your tab's in the book." Gus: "Anyway. Coffee?" Ray: "Buddy." Pete: your name.

## Why it works
A bookend that the player composes by playing. The mirror makes leaving a choice about what you're leaving, and the line from your creditor makes the cash-out feel like what it is in this city: walking out on a marker. The returning save then starts the morning after, with the sign in front of you again.

## How the player experiences it
One new plate (the bridge from the driver's seat, mirror inset) with the sign swapped in by state. The rest of the epilogue builder runs as settled.

## Risks and tradeoffs
Six or seven mirror variants is a small art list. The "line from the person you owe" needs one line per key NPC; the voice sheets already supply them.

## Decision notes
- Mike, 2026-10-27: rejected, and the drive-away is cut from the game: "At the end you stay there and run the new casino/hotel." See bible 4.1.8.
