## Summary
The docs' best small device is Dot and Gus each asking you how the other is doing. Extend it to a layer: a dozen pairs who can't or won't speak to each other and ask the player instead. One line each, fired as random events once a season, and the player becomes the city's go-between.

## Current state
"Dot and Gus each ask about the other" (A1.7, pages/storymap-beats.md; the bible's OQ 20 notes it sits oddly with Dot's "never talks about Gus"). Pete is "a quiet go-between for Dot and Gus." No other pair asks.

## Proposed change
A pool of "ask about" lines, one per pair, each a random event at the asker's venue, gated on the player having met both, cooldown a season, no choice (or one: tell them the truth or a kind version, which bluffs on information):

| Asker | About | The line |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Pete | Rosa | "You see her? ... Don't tell her I asked." |
| Rosa | Pete | "Is he still wearing the vest buttoned. Good." |
| Stella | Dot | "Does Aunt Dot still do the voice when she tells the flood story? ... My father hasn't heard it in years." |
| Tae | Gus | "How's his color. Never mind. Don't answer that." |
| Richard | Bennett | "My boy ran for class president once. Peeked at the ballots too, I expect." (Only ever the boy; never the man.) |
| Bennett | Richard | Never asks. That's the line: if the player brings Richard up, "Go to bed." |
| Kasia | Juan | "Tell Juan his Tuesday crowd owes me for the coupons. He'll know it's a joke. Tell him anyway." |
| Juan | Kasia | "She still keeping that book? Tell her lane six says hi." |
| Raffy | Jalen | "He okay? He doesn't text back." (S-027) |
| Jalen | Raffy | "Is Raffy still doing deliveries, sir? ... Bikes. Okay." (He suspects; S-027 keeps him from knowing.) |
| Vivian | Tae | "Is the boy still buying up the lakefront? Tell him the lake's not for sale. He'll know what I mean." (S-033) |
| Gus | Dot | As written, and never says her sister's name. |
| Dot | Gus | As written, and resolves OQ 20: she doesn't *talk* about Gus; she asks about him, once a season, and changes the subject before you answer. |

## Why it works
It's the ensemble technique the docs are missing: interconnection shown through what people can't say to each other. Thirteen lines make the city feel like one place and make the player the thread through it, which is the blank-slate protagonist's best role. It also quietly tells the player who's estranged from whom before any mystery explains why.

## How the player experiences it
A line at a break point, now and then, with a bluff-or-truth choice where it matters. Nothing is tracked on screen.

## Risks and tradeoffs
None in art. The lines must stay rare (once a season per pair) or they become a chore. Bennett's non-ask should be the only pair that never fires.
