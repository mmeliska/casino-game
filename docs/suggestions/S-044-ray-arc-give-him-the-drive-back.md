## Summary
State Ray's want (to be the man he's dressed as) and need (to get out from under Wren before she lets him finish digging), add a choice no doc offers (return the drive to Ray, knowing what it is), and give his epilogue two ends.

## Current state
Ray: mid-40s, "trying a bit too hard," answers to Wren (a sore spot), the go-between who never knows the whole plan; made a secret insurance copy of Wren's logs that Raffy stole by mistake; "can only hunt for it quietly"; "lends too much," makes Kiran skim, "thinks Wren doesn't know. She does, and lets him dig his own hole. He shrinks when the real Umbra shows up" (people/ray.md). A1.5's choices: return the envelope to Ray, keep the cash, hand the drive to Ed, help Raffy pay him back. S11 gives the player leverage on Ray.

## Proposed change
**Want:** to be the car, the coat and the ring. **Need:** out. He made the drive because some part of him knows Wren will finish him; it's the only smart thing he's ever done and he can't tell anyone.

**The new choice.** At A1.5 "return it to Ray" currently returns the envelope. Add a variant available only if the player opened it: hand him the drive and say you looked. Pose swap: arms-wide to hands in pockets (the review's tell). He's a different man for one panel. He never thanks you and never forgets. Effects: his loans to you carry no vig, ever; at A4.7 "loose ends" there's a new option, "leverage on Ray" becomes "Ray owes you," which pays as a favor instead of a threat; and if the player later needs someone to get Gus out of the Cinder Room at A4.2, Ray walks him out himself, which is the only decent thing Ray does in the game and the player caused it.

**Arc stages:**
1. The pitch (act 1): the password, the loan, "buddy."
2. The squeeze (act 2): Wren's crew moves in; he's louder than before, which is the tell.
3. Blackspruce (winter): the real Umbra at the table; he shrinks in front of you; if you hold his secret he's the one who makes sure you leave with your money.
4. Loose ends (act 4): the plate from S11, the skim, the drive. He is the most leverageable man in the game and knows it.

**Epilogue, two ends:** if Wren falls (A4.3, by the drive if you kept it or by the Aurelian footage if you gave the drive back), Ray's front page is either a man testifying (if the S11 plate put him at the lawyer's meeting and you gave it to Ed) or a man who left town the week before. If Wren stands, he's still at the Flare's bar in whatever the Flare became, a little older, the car gone, and he says "buddy" to you and means it as a question.

## Why it works
The hub character of the debt system has no mercy in him and none available to the player; adding one choice that treats him as a person gives the loan shark a face and the player a kind of power the game doesn't otherwise offer: knowing something about someone and not using it. (Technique: unexpected mercy, reciprocity.) It also closes a logical gap: a player who opened the envelope already knows Ray's secret, and the docs never let them say so.

## How the player experiences it
One extra option at A1.5 for players who looked; a pose swap; a quieter Ray afterward; and one scene at A4.2 where the loudest man in the game is the one who gets Gus out.

## Risks and tradeoffs
Giving Ray the drive removes the A4.3 clean proof (the logs) from the player's hands; that's the cost, and it should be stated in the option's stake ("Hand Ray the drive. You won't get it back."). The Aurelian footage route (Felix/Nora) still exists for exposing Umbra.
