## Summary
Replace staking with a tuition loan (Mike, 2026-10-28). Jalen's tuition comes due and he's short. He won't ask. If the player notices and offers, the loan goes in the Wallet as "what Jalen owes you," and he repays it in tip envelopes. In act 4 he comes into an inheritance and decides on his own to become a backer of the new Starflare.

## Current state
"You can stake him, his wins pay you back, and your choices decide whether he climbs clean. He gets out of Backwater, pays back his stake in act 4, and becomes one of only two backers" (people/jalen.md). The Wallet lists "what Jalen owes you" (pages/gdd.md). S-029 makes him a hospitality student who works the Flare at night for tuition; staking is dropped (Mike, 2026-10-28).

## Proposed change
**The tuition bill (act 1 or 2, once you've met him at A1.7).** A reveal-type event at the Flare. Insert: a college billing notice tucked under the busboy tray, the amount due circled (a few hundred dollars, tuned to an act 1 bankroll). Jalen sees you see it and puts the tray down on top of it. "It's handled. Thank you, sir." It isn't. ("Sir" follows the player's register title.)

The player can:
- **Offer the loan.** He says no once. If you offer again, he takes it and writes the amount on a napkin: "I'll have it back to you." The Wallet shows "what Jalen owes you." No interest, no due date; he sets his own.
- **Say nothing.** He covers it himself: double shifts, and he's on the floor every night for a season, tired and polite. He sits out the next term. Nothing else is lost, and he never mentions it.

**The envelopes.** If you lent it, envelopes appear in your Pockets over the next in-game weeks: $20, $35, $15, busboy tips, until it's repaid to the dollar. The last one has a note: "Thank you, sir. Midterms went fine." (The honorific follows the register here too.) He never says a word about it on the floor. That's the character.

**The inheritance (act 4).** A great-aunt he met twice leaves him her savings. Nobody else in the cast is involved. Jalen decides on his own to put it into the new Starflare, which is how he becomes one of its two backers. If you lent him tuition, the first thing he does is try to pay you again; you can refuse it, and he takes that as an answer. If you didn't, he just invests.

## Why it works
It keeps the player in Jalen's story through generosity instead of gambling, which fits a kid who wants to run a hotel, not a poker table. The envelopes are the cheapest emotional mechanic in the game (inventory as character). The inheritance gives him backer money without tying him to anyone, and his choice to invest is his own, so his rise isn't the player's to buy.

## How the player experiences it
A billing-notice insert; one offer; a napkin IOU; envelopes in Pockets; in act 4, a lawyer's letter folded in a textbook and the news that he's in.

## Risks and tradeoffs
Removes staking from the docs: "you stake him," "his wins pay you back" and "pays back his stake in act 4" change in `people/jalen.md` later. "What Jalen owes you" in the Wallet stays, now as a loan. An inheritance can feel convenient; keep it small on screen (one letter, one line) and late enough (act 4) that the player has already seen him work for every dollar.
