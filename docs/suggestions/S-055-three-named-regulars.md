## Summary
The Flare's regulars carry the people's Starflare (O4), the scatter (A3.5), the shoestring offer (A4.10) and half the seeds, but they're a crowd. Name three, give each one line and one seat, and let them age with the Flare. Each gets a portrait (Mike, 2026-10-28).

## Current state
"The regulars" appear in A1.7, A3.5, A4.10, O4, S2 (the mill pensioner), S3 (the trucker), and the review's placeholder "Walt" (pages/review.md: "name not checked for clashes"). The walk-on table lists the pensioner and the trucker without names (pages/walkons.md). Portraits are budgeted for core and supporting only.

## Proposed change
Three regulars, each with a portrait at the supporting-character standard, seen at a Flare table or the bar:

| Name | Seat | The line | What they carry |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Walt (keep the review's placeholder if it clears; the review put him on the Prairie Queen, so he's a regular of both) | Blackjack, third base, every night | "Boat's cheap tables count short. Not for you. For the house. Go look." | The one who sends you to the Prairie Queen (M3's floor-manager grumble, relayed); the people's Starflare's first shareholder |
| Lorraine | The Rocket Wheel, Fridays, $5 | "I've lost on this wheel since it was new. It owes me." | The mill pensioner's wife (S2); the one who brings the Flare's regulars to the hearing to stand beside the league (S1) |
| Augie | The bar, a coffee, never plays | "I come for the band. There's no band. I still come." | The one who tells you about the covered piano; the last to leave on the last night |

Rules: they age with the plate (Augie's stool is empty one night in act 3; Lorraine moves to the RailYard after the scatter and complains about the wheel there; Walt is at the union hall in O4 with a share). They never get a scene of their own; they get a line in someone else's, with their portrait in the two-shot. Pete knows their ratings by heart, which is how the player learns their names.

## Why it works
The people's Starflare needs people. Three names the player hears in act 1 become three faces at the hearing and the union hall, and "the regulars scatter" becomes "Augie's stool is empty." It's the cheapest belonging the game can buy, and it gives the scatter and the shoestring offer the specific loss and specific loyalty they currently lack.

## How the player experiences it
Lines at the table with a portrait. Absence as a plate state: an empty stool where a face used to be.

## Risks and tradeoffs
Names need a clash check (Walt flagged already). Three portraits add to the budget in `plan/t06.md` (19 now; supporting tier is $20 to $80 each, so $60 to $240). Three is the cap. Each portrait needs one or two poses only, since they never lead a scene.
