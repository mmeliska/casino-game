## Summary
Give Gus's late wife a name, Alice, said first by Dot, never by Gus until the end. Make "her touches are all over the Flare" three specific things the player can find: the piano, the planetarium lounge's ceiling, and the chipped mug.

## Current state
"His late wife's touches are all over the Flare, which is why he can't let go; she was Dot's sister" (people/gus.md); offstage, unnamed (pages/walkons.md). "The piano was his late wife's pick" (people/miles.md, beat 2). A "planetarium lounge" is in the Flare's first description on the stale pages/direction.md and nowhere else; this suggestion revives it as the lounge's ceiling. Gus's chipped rocket mug is his signature prop. Dot is "a play on dice" (pages/notebook.md).

## Proposed change
**The name.** Alice (Mike, 2026-10-27). Rule for writers: Dot says "Alice" freely once she trusts you; Gus says "she" and "my wife" all game and says "Alice" exactly once, at the end (opening night, or the 2am walk if it ended badly, S-011). Stella says "Mom."

**Three touches**, each an insert the player can find, no caption:
1. **The piano.** Alice chose it; Miles/Audrey already knows this. As the Flare declines it gets covered. When Miles/Audrey's beat 2 fires, the line becomes "Alice's piano. Gus won't say her name, so I play it instead."
2. **The ceiling.** The planetarium lounge's painted stars are hers: she repainted the rocket-age ceiling herself the first winter they were married, and got one constellation wrong on purpose so she'd know it was hers. Insert: the wrong constellation. Stella points it out when she's telling a story about her dad; the player can later notice it reproduced on the new Starflare's ceiling (A5.4 sign-design scene offers it as a line item at no cost).
3. **The mug.** Chipped the night she died: Gus dropped it in the Cinder Room when he finally looked at the phone (S-012). He has drunk from the chip every day since. Nobody says this; the player who has S-012's reveal will understand the insert they've seen a hundred times.

## Why it works
"Her touches are everywhere" is exposition; three findable things are environmental storytelling, which the writing rules ask for. A name said by the sister and withheld by the husband is a verbal tell that pays off once. The ceiling gives the rebuild a reason to be hers as well as his father's, which softens the "dead man's jacket" problem in S-011.

## How the player experiences it
Three inserts across acts 1 and 2; Dot's name-drop once she trusts you; Gus's single use of the name at the end. The ceiling line item in A5.4.

## Risks and tradeoffs
The name is settled (Alice). The mug chip depends on S-012 for its meaning but works as a prop without it (a soft link; S-012 lists this file, so this file can't list S-012).
